# This statement changed the game for me

Hey {first_name},




Take a moment to consider this: you are the writer, director, producer, and actor of your life.




Sit with that for just a moment.





It's a powerful realization, is it not?




When I first contemplated this idea, I experienced two contrasting emotions simultaneously.




On one hand, I felt empowered beyond measure.




Yet, on the other hand, I felt a surge of responsibility wash over me.




The realization dawned on me that it's entirely up to me to craft the life I deeply want.




A life that I am willing to fight for.




Upon further introspection, I noticed how profoundly the language we use, both with ourselves and others, impacts our ability to shape and live our lives to play all out.




There are common sentiments lately I often hear expressed:

"I'm just getting old..."





"I'm always tired..."





"I've learned to live with the pain..."





"I’ll do that (insert anything for yourself) next week."




It's easy to slip into these patterns of thought and speech, especially when bombarded with mainstream narratives about health, fitness, and what's achievable when we prioritize our well-being.




But here's the crux of the matter—it doesn't have to be this way.




You have the power to script your own narrative.




This isn't about denying the reality of your current circumstances or struggles. It's about acknowledging them and taking ownership of the life you're creating and have allowed to be created—in your work, relationships, health, and every other aspect.




Granted, it may not always seem fair, and that's okay.




I encourage you to embrace those feelings and sensations fully. That is deep work by itself.




Then, from a place of greater clarity and authenticity, take the next step forward.




Sometimes that's all you need to feel into and know.




Just the next step.




Remember, your life is your masterpiece, waiting to be written, directed, produced, and performed by you.




With respect,

Mike
