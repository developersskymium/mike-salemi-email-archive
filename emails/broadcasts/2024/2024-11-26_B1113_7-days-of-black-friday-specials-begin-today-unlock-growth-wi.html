What Struggles Does the Enneagram Help With?

Each Enneagram type experiences life through a specific lens, which can reveal both strengths and struggles.


Here’s a snapshot of the challenges each type may face:

  • Type 1 (The Improver): Struggles with self-criticism, perfectionism, and always feeling like they “should” be doing more.
  • Type 2 (The Helper): Often prioritizes others' needs to feel loved, leading to burnout and resentment.
  • Type 3 (The Achiever): Measures worth by productivity and success, struggling to slow down or feel fulfilled.
  • Type 4 (The Individualist): Can feel stuck in comparison and self-doubt, longing for authenticity yet fearing they’ll never find it.
  • Type 5 (The Investigator): May withdraw to conserve energy, struggling to connect deeply or trust their contributions are enough.
  • Type 6 (The Loyalist): Worries about worst-case scenarios and seeks external security, often at the expense of their inner peace.
  • Type 7 (The Enthusiast): Avoids pain through distraction, constantly chasing the next adventure while struggling with commitment or discipline.
  • Type 8 (The Challenger): Protects themselves by being overly strong or dominant, but may struggle with vulnerability and trust.
  • Type 9 (The Peacemaker): Avoids conflict by minimizing their own needs, often feeling unseen or disconnected from their purpose.

Through the Enneagram, we’ll uncover how these patterns are showing up for you, what you're looking to get most out of our sesison together, and create a plan to help you grow beyond these patterns and challenges showing in your life.
