  • Midlife Crisis and the Wise Man/Woman Archetype: Understanding the choice point as an opportunity for spiritual growth and the transition to wisdom.

Paul’s insights are as deep and impactful as ever, and this conversation is packed with practical advice, powerful stories, and a lot of heart.
