# [🎙️EP #120]  12 Part Framework: "The Archetype Wheel" with Greg Schmaus

Hi {first_name},




In this episode, author, coach, and returning guest Greg Schmaus and I dive into the world of archetypes and their role in personal development. We explore how our lives mirror stories, with each of us embodying different archetypes, like the hero, across various areas such as work and relationships.




You'll learn about The Archetype Wheel, which links these archetypes to the 12 astrological houses, providing a framework for understanding personal patterns and challenges. The conversation also covers survival archetypes like the victim, saboteur, prostitute, and child, and how they can impact our behaviors. Greg shares insights on self-resourcing, and how understanding these archetypes can lead to more powerful and balanced lives.
