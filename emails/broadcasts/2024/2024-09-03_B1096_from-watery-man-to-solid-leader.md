# From "Watery" Man to Solid Leader

Hi {first_name},

 

Ever heard, or felt this, before about yourself, or a man you know?

 

Being a "watery" man in life can mean multiple things...being unreliable, frequently changing plans, failing to honor commitments, and often getting lost in distractions or emotions are a few.

It suggests a lack of discipline, structure, and grounding.

I, too, have had moments of "wateryness." Times when I failed to uphold my schedule or overcommitted myself, leading to disappointments and missed responsibilities. 

 

It's important to acknowledge that unforeseen circumstances can disrupt our plans, BUT what I'm addressing is a deeper, more pervasive pattern.

 

This is about not honoring our commitments, not taking full responsibility for our actions, and letting our words diverge from our deeds. 

 

Many men feel ashamed of this, leading them to shrink, hide, or escape, pretending nothing happened.

 

It can be tough to take responsibility and accountability for these behaviors, I get it, but it's also true and very likely that this pattern has persisted for too long, leaving those close to you craving more reliability and leadership.

 

Watery men are difficult to trust. They are often unreliable in many aspects of life. 

 

The path to change this pattern is clear:

DECIDE you want and are ready for something different - don’t BS yourself here.
SEEK out other men who can hold you accountable, and even guide you through this. 
COMMIT to the practice of transforming these behaviors, and more important what is driving them.

While this journey isn't easy, it's definitely possible. 

I've seen it in myself and in many other men—the shift from being unreliable to becoming a solid, clear, and action-oriented man.

 

This transformation is at the core of my upcoming Path to Inspired Action 8-week Online Group Men's Program, starting in just 3 weeks. My mission is to help men step into their greatness by getting honest about where they're playing small and causing harm to themselves and others.

 

There's no judgment here. If you've recognized "watery" patterns in your life and want to change this story and become a more trustworthy, confident, and empowered man, I'm here to support.
