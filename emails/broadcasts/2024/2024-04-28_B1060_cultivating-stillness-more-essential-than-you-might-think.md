# Cultivating Stillness: more essential than you might think…

Hey {first_name},

  

I hear it from men nearly every day - they feel pulled in countless directions.

 

Work obligations.

 

Making money.

 

Quality time with their intimate partner.

 

Self-care.

 

Nurturing friendships.

 

Time with their children.

 

The list goes on.

 

For all these reasons, and more, I find it paramount to spend time cultivating a practice of stillness - which is not synonymous with meditation btw.

 

Simply, stillness.

It might be taking a minute to stop to observe the nature around us.

 

It might be taking a pause to stop and listen for the farthest sound you can hear.

 

It might be resting to feel and hear your own heart beat.

 

It might be stopping for a moment, remembering to breathe so that we can regulate our racing minds.

 

Stillness is nourishment.

 

In fact, I believe that many kids today, and even many adults, simply can’t sit still.

 

And many have zero capacity for boredom.

 

Being bored I feel, is actually one of the most underrated gifts we can give ourselves.

 

So what happens then if we don't intentionally create time for nothing, to be still?

 

Simply put, we burn out.

 

Or we become like powder kegs on the inside, waiting to explode.

 

We may appear superficially as stoic, or like we have it all together, but deep down, we know the truth.

 

And here’s the thing I've learned from my own painful experiences: if we don’t stop to be still when we have the conscious choice, life will force us to do so at some point or another.

 

And it doesn’t take much—far less than you might think.

 

Take 10 minutes today. It’s Sunday. Simply be still.

 

Don’t overcomplicate it. Just sit, listen, observe.

 

From this place, I’m willing to bet that everything will become clearer.

 

You will know what you need to do next.

 

You will love more deeply.

 

You will feel more vital.

 

Give yourself this gift.

 

With love,

Mike
