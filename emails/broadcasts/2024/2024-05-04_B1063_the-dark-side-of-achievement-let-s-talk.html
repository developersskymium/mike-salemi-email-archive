B1063 · The Dark Side of Achievement: Let's Talk

Hey {first_name},

 

Ever find yourself chasing one success after another, only to wonder why the fulfillment never quite arrives?

 

Trust me, you're not alone.

 

In a world where success is often defined by your bank account or your latest accomplishment, it's easy to get caught up in the chase. And don't get me wrong, I'm all for setting goals and kicking butt. But there's a flip side to all this hustle that we don't talk about enough.

 

Take it from someone who's been there (and chats with men frequently on this struggle): the pursuit of success can be an illusion and can come from a deeper, hidden place—a place that whispers, "you're worthless unless you're constantly proving yourself, doing, or achieving something." Sound familiar?

 

I remember when, for SO long, I only felt valued and happy when I hit a won a competition, or when there was a certain weight on the bar. If I didn't hit my goals or achieve, I would go to a dark place inside, and also, as a result of being an achievement fanatic, there were many costs (namely relationships) that suffered.

 

When we tie our worth solely to what we achieve, we miss out on the essence of who we are. We start seeking validation outside ourselves, which can wreak havoc on our relationships and leave us feeling hollow, even when we've ticked off all the boxes society tells us we should.

 

So, what's the antidote?

 

No, it's not giving up on your dreams or pretending achievements don't matter. It's about digging deep and asking yourself some tough questions. Like, why are you really chasing those goals? Who are you doing it for? And most importantly, can you find fulfillment and worthiness within yourself, regardless of the accolades?
