This particular retreat focused on delivering a personalized deep dive into increasing the men’s life force, and unlocking the power that lay dormant inside.

 

And after everyone had left on Sunday, and while the facilitators and I were cleaning up, I had the opportunity to first get some hands-on bodywork on my knee by Soma Therapist and facilitator, Justin Brien.

 

For a little context before going further - my knee has been in some degree of pain off and on, and to varying degrees, for nearly 7 years.

 

It’s a repetitive stress injury to my LCL. I’ve known how to manage it, but I’ve not experienced full resolution, especially in times when I’m not diligent with my maintenance program, or when I push my body hard in training.

 

I know full well there is a mechanical issue going in and around the knee, and yet I’ve always believed there was more to the story - especially after years of doing this type of men's work, and considering the amount of rehab I’ve already done on it.

 

You see, I’ve continuously been curious regarding what pain symbolizes and what it can point us towards.

 

My knee pain is on the right side, which is associated with the masculine principle.

 

Additionally, depending on the source you read, one theme of the knees is our willingness to concede or resist life. For instance, one needs to surrender in life in order to bend and move with fluidity and also to concede one’s position.

 

Knee pain can hint at times or situations where we are stubborn, prideful, inflexible, obstinate, or self-righteous.

 

Being very honest with you, my tendency towards rigidness and my harsh critic to live up to the perception of family values of success, I imagine are highly connected to it. It's a heavy weight I've beared for a long time.

 

To go further, the left knee is more indicative of moving forward; the right knee is indicative of judgments of actions taken or not taken in the moving forward process.

 

Time and time again, I have been surprised by the underlying theme, blockage, trauma, story, or whatever you call it, connected to the physical pain in my body.

 

This has been true in my life, as well as for clients.

 

Remember, pain is inevitable; that’s just a fact of life.

 

However, our resistance to pain is what makes it suffering.

 

And often, pain is there to teach us something.

 

Now, with that context, back to the story…

 

After Justin had treated my knee, Dr. Jonny Suarez, another legit facilitator at the event, gave me one highly specific rehab exercise to do, just one.

 

Immediately, I could tell that something big was shifting.

 

I’ve done countless exercises over the years, and since the retreat, I’ve been shocked that 99% of my knee pain is gone - GONE. It’s been nearly 10 days and I am walking down stairs pain-free, doing lateral lunges pain-free, and more. Thank you Dr. Jonny!

 

To top this off, two days later, a man in my life, who I hold very dear, said something to me that I don't recall him saying before.

 

He told me he was proud of me.

 

Now, I'm well aware of the self-seduction that can come from placing our worth on external validation. That said, this one hit very close to home for reasons I won’t share here - suffice to say, it meant a LOT.

 

Additionally, I started seeing major takeoffs in my business as well.

 

I’ve been borderline baffled.
