# [🎙️NEW EP.]  The Food Dilemma: Our Relationship to Food

Hi {first_name},




The conversation explores the relationship to food and how it evolves over time. Food Educator, Holistic Health Coach, and Private Chef Lauren Rose Salemi discusses her upbringing in a health-conscious household and the impact it had on her food choices. Lauren and Mike also reflect on the challenges of healthy eating in today's society and the perception of extreme diets. The importance of conscious food sourcing and the benefits of growing one's own food are highlighted.  The conversation also touches on the deceptive nature of appearances and the importance of listening to our bodies and making adjustments when necessary.
