# Brotherhood in the Wild: Explore Sacred Hunting with Me in Texas

Hi {first_name},




I am honored to extend a personal invitation to a special upcoming event – our Sacred Hunting Experience in the heart of Texas from February 29th to March 3rd.




Sacred Rituals:


This is not just a hunting trip; it is a carefully crafted series of sacred rituals meant to guide you on a profound journey of self-discovery and transformation. Held in the landscape of Texas, this experience is an opportunity to reconnect with your primal essence and tap into the wisdom of the natural world. One of these rituals is a private Sweat Lodge, and I will discuss a few of the others below.




Men's Only Container:


In the spirit of fostering authentic connections and brotherhood, our Sacred Hunting Experience is a men's only event. This intentional container creates a space where participants can freely express themselves, forge deep connections, and embark on a shared journey of growth.




Sacred Plant Medicine:


Part of this approach is the mindful use of sacred plant medicine, artfully integrated to enhance the spiritual and transformative aspects of the journey. This practice serves as a gateway to self-awareness, offering a unique opportunity for personal reflection and communion with the natural world. Your guide for this will be Mansal Denton.




Beyond the Hunt


While hunting is a pivotal part of the experience, our journey encompasses much more. From mindfulness practices to group discussions and guided reflections, we offer a holistic exploration of your identity, purpose, and potential.




Spot and Stalk Hunting


The style of hunting on this trip employs the "spot and stalk" method—a practice that combines observation and patience to connect with the wild. This method is not only in alignment with ethical hunting principles but also provides a beginner-friendly introduction to the art of hunting. We will be on the move, following the animals as we hunt.




Ideal for Beginners


Whether you're a seasoned hunter or a complete novice, our experience is tailored to meet you where you are. Expert guides, including Mansal Denton, Mike Bledsoe, and others, will provide comprehensive support and guidance, ensuring that every participant feels empowered and comfortable throughout the trip. Also, note that this is a rifle hunt.




Bonuses


To enhance your preparation for this unique experience, everyone attending will receive complimentary access to my new Bulgarian Bag online program. This program is designed to physically prepare you for the challenges of the hunt, promoting strength, agility, and resilience.




In addition to the physical preparation, I am stoked to announce that I will be guiding breathwork at various points throughout the trip. This will be a powerful complement to our experience together.
