# The Unfair Truth About Masculine Leadership in Relationships

Hey {first_name},

 

Ever been hit with that gut punch of responsibility, especially when you're trying to be the rock in your relationship?

 

Here's the raw truth: being a real leader in a relationship isn't for the faint-hearted. It can sometimes feel like you're diving into a battlefield, no matter how heavy the odds, leadership in the moment falls on you.

 

When life's smooth sailing, sure, it's a breeze basically. But when things hit the fan, and emotions run wild, that's when the real test begins.

 

And here's the kicker: even when it's not your fault, if you aim to embody good masculine leadership in the relationship, then it's on you to step up and lead, that's just the way it is.

 

Now, I'm not trying to preach to you. But if you want to be the kind of man who steers the ship, it's time to roll up your sleeves and own it.

Here's the deal: using your masculine energy in leadership means utilizing structure to create an intentional space where the flow of energy can move as optimally as possible.

 

That might mean setting a time for you and your partner to have a heart-to-heart, leading with a helpful communication framework such as Nonviolent Communication or the Imago Dialogue, or just creating an environment where you both feel safe to speak your minds and hearts.

 

Even if life throws you a curveball, it's still on whoever is "holding the masculine" in a relationship to step up to the plate and swing.

 

I know it's a lot to take in.

 

My invitation would be to sit with this, and if it resonates, then go through a check-in/evaluation of how your current situation, or future challenge could be lead in that very moment with more structure, presence, intention, and breath.

 

Leaning In,

Mike
