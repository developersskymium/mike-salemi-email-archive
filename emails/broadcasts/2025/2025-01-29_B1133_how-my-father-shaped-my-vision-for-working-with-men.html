B1133 · How My Father Shaped My Vision for Working with Men

Hi {first_name},

 

Growing up with an immigrant father, I was shaped by his relentless drive and the example he set through his work. My dad didn’t just go through life; he built it. From pulling espresso shots while standing on a milk crate at the age of 9 in Sicily, to becoming the head pastry chef at a busy Italian grocery store when he moved to the States, he embodied what it meant to create something with his own hands.

 

But it wasn’t just about business. It was about carving a path, no matter the odds, and doing it with integrity. I saw that firsthand. He was always willing to disrupt the status quo, and whether it was working in the kitchen, running a landscaping business, or building a marble and granite company, he put in the work—no shortcuts. It wasn’t just for him—it was for his family.

 

What I really took from him wasn’t just the business work ethic. It was the commitment to craft, the courage to take risks, and the unwavering determination to provide and protect what matters most. He taught me the value of building something meaningful, even when the world says it's easier to take a more predictable route.

 

This week, as I prepare to launch the Vital Man Collective, I’m reminded of the impact my father had on me as a man. Over the last five years of focusing on men’s work—leading retreats, running online programs, coaching men one-on-one, and participating in various men's groups and immersive experiences—I’ve seen firsthand some of the challenges men face when entering this work.

 

For one, what exactly is "men’s work"? Ask 100 different people, and you’ll likely get 100 different answers. I, too, have contributed to this confusion.

 

In my experience, I’ve found a few common barriers that prevent men from getting the support they need:

  • High upfront price points that make it difficult to access the value of this work and determine if it's a good fit for them.
  • Start dates for programs that often don’t align with when men need help the most.
  • A lack of ongoing accountability, support, and community after a program/group ends.
  • "Leaky" containers where men don’t feel supported enough to open up and dive deep.
  • The lack of action. Awareness is great, but if your life doesn’t change, what's the point?

 

After countless hours of reflection and discussion, I’ve come to a conclusion. The solution is the Vital Man Collective.

 

This is a unique blend of a deep, supportive community, a men's group, access to all of my past work (programs, workshops, etc.), and everything I create moving forward!

 

I will tell you straight up, the men who join the VMC will regularly be challenged - however, I can assure you it will come from a place of respect, love, and a desire to see each man lead their life in the biggest way possible.

 

The VMC for men who are ready to step into their authentic power with integrity and alignment—free from burnout and the constant need for external validation.
