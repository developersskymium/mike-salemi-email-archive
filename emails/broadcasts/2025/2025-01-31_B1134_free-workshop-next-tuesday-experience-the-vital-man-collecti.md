A lot of men pride themselves on doing a lot. They take on more responsibilities, push harder in their careers, train their bodies, and aim to be great partners, fathers, and leaders.

A New Relationship with Challenge – Develop a mindset where resistance becomes fuel, rather than something that drains you.
