# What Weights Can't Fix

Hi {first_name},




I grew up in the gym.




It’s where I felt strong. Safe. In control.
And for most of my life, movement has been my medicine.




It’s helped me process pain, regulate stress, stay focused, and build the kind of discipline I’m proud of.




But I’ve also used it to avoid what I wasn’t ready to face.




There were times I’d be in the gym 6–7 days a week—not because my body needed it, but because I didn’t know how to be with the stuff underneath.
The restlessness. The grief. The anger I didn’t have words for.
Movement was my way to not feel those things. And on the surface, it looked healthy.




But here’s what I’ve learned:
Even something as powerful as training can become an escape when we don’t know what to do with our inner world.




This isn’t a knock on working out—I still train hard, and I’ll always believe in the power of physical expression.




But I’m also asking better questions now. Questions like:

Am I moving to connect… or to avoid?
What’s the emotional charge I’m carrying into this workout?
What’s one thing I haven’t slowed down enough to feel this week?
If I had no training today, where would my tension go?

These aren’t comfortable questions—but they’re the ones that change everything.




Because your vitality isn’t just in your deadlift.


It’s in your presence. Your honesty. Your ability to feel and not flinch.




This is the work we do inside The Vital Man Collective. Movement. Breath. Language. Embodiment. And a brotherhood that doesn’t just spot you in the gym—but in life.
