B1162 · You weren’t made for a world of noise.

Hi {first_name},


There’s a reason you feel more tired after scrolling your phone.
There’s a reason you feel more restless after spending a day indoors.


We weren’t designed for the constant noise we live in today — the notifications, the deadlines, the bright screens, the endless inputs.
We were built for the rhythms of the earth.
For sunrises and seasons. For breath and movement. For real connection — to the land, to each other, and to ourselves.


Tim Corcoran, who I’m honored to co-lead my upcoming retreat with, has spent decades guiding men back to these natural rhythms.
He’s the founder of Headwaters Outdoor School in Mt. Shasta and the author of The Earth Caretaker Way — but what matters most is that Tim walks the path himself — rooted, humble, and deeply connected to the land.


He teaches that the body has a memory older than civilization.
It remembers the language of the wind.
It knows the feeling of belonging to the land — not owning it, not managing it, but being a part of it.


When we stay too long in the human-made world, we lose touch with this ancient memory. We begin to mistake busyness for purpose, stimulation for connection, and comfort for vitality.


When life gets too loud, it’s easy to think we need to do more to fix it.

Sometimes, the real medicine is not doing more — it’s creating enough space to finally hear yourself again.

Nature doesn’t yell.
It doesn’t rush.
It doesn’t perform.
It simply is — and when we’re close enough, we start to remember that we are too.

And when you taste that kind of clarity again, it changes the way you move through the rest of your life.


In Health & Happiness,


Mike Salemi
Performance Specialist, Men's Leadership Coach, & Host of The Path Podcast
