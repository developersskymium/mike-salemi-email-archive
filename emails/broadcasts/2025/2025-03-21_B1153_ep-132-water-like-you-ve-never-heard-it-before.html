In this episode of The Path with Mike Salemi, I sit down with Scott Estrada for a deeply personal and powerful conversation on healing, hydration, and the miracle of his father’s recovery.


After a traumatic spinal cord injury left Scott’s father with a grim prognosis, Scott devoted himself to creating a healing environment using everything he had learned over 40 years of studying human biology, hydration, and natural medicine. What unfolded inside that hospital room defied what most would consider possible.


We dive into the science and spirit behind real healing—how structured water, mineralization, light, and nature all play a role in restoring the body at a cellular level. Scott also shares what it took to make peace with his father’s choices and how surrender, presence, and deep trust became part of the healing process.


Whether you’re into high-performance, holistic health, or the emotional journey of transformation—this conversation will move you.​​​​​​​
