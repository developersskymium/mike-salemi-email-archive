# What Most Men Never Got Growing Up

Hi {first_name},

 

We don’t talk enough about the role of elders.


Not just older men—but true elders.

 

The kind of men who’ve spent decades listening to the land.


Who speak with clarity because they’ve been shaped by fire, failure, and solitude.


Men who carry stories that don’t come from books—but from experience.

In traditional cultures, elders weren’t just advisors.


They were initiators. Witnesses. Truth tellers.


They were the ones who marked the turning point between boyhood and manhood—not with shame, but with honor.

 

And for most of us… that moment never came.

 

There’s a story from Lakota tradition about Crazy Horse. As a young man, he went into the wilderness alone for a vision quest. Four days—no food, no distractions—just him, the land, and his prayers. On the final day, he was given a vision that shaped the man he would become: a humble, untouchable protector of his people.

 

When he returned, his community welcomed him back—not just as a boy, but as a man. That kind of recognition lives in the bones.

 

We don’t grow like that today.


But we can.

 

That’s why I'd like to share something with you.

 

There’s a man I know and highly respect—Tim Corcoran, founder of Headwaters Outdoor School.


Tim is what most men have never experienced: a real elder.

 

He’s lived in deep relationship with the land for over six decades.


He’s mentored thousands—young and old—through earth-based teachings, ancestral skills, and personal transformation rooted in nature.

Tim doesn’t need to impress anyone. He’s walked a path of simplicity, strength, and clarity—and you can feel it the moment you meet him. He teaches by presence more than words. And when he does speak, you listen.

 

This spring, I’ve teamed up with  Tim to guide a group of men in a powerful weekend immersion in Mt. Shasta on his sacred land.

 

This experience is a return...a return to something deeper.

 

And if you’ve never had the opportunity to sit with an elder… it’s time.

 

Reply to this email with "ELDER" and I'll get you more info on our May 17-20 special event with Tim and me.

 

In Health & Happiness,

 

Mike Salemi
Performance Specialist, Men's Leadership Coach, & Host of The Path Podcast
