B1129 · The Hidden Power of Time Blocking

Hi {first_name},

 

A few weeks ago, I found myself sitting at my desk, staring at a to-do list that seemed to grow every time I blinked. I had emails to respond to, social media notifications piling up, and half a dozen “urgent” tasks pulling me in different directions.

 

I started working on one thing, then another, but nothing was actually getting done. By the end of the day, I was exhausted, frustrated, and questioning where all my time had gone.

 

One of the biggest frustrations I’ve had with myself over the years is this: I know I’m a hard worker, and I can get a lot of stuff done. But I’ve realized that busyness doesn’t equal success. I'll say that again: busyness doesn't equal success. That was a really tough pill for me to swallow and sit with if I'm being totally honest right now.

 

It’s discernment and consistent, focused action that truly moves the needle.

 

That night, I picked up The One Thing  - a book I recently shared in one of my prior emails to you discussing the myth of multitasking. One concept immediately grabbed my attention: time blocking. I realized I wasn’t just scattered—I was leaking energy.

 

Distraction had become the enemy of my focus, my presence, and ultimately, my purpose.

 

Since then, I’ve made time blocking a daily practice. It’s simple, but it’s not always easy. The idea is to dedicate uninterrupted time to your one thing—the task that makes everything else easier or unnecessary.

 

For me, the key shift was asking this question: What’s the ONE thing I can do right now that would make the biggest difference?

 

At first, the book's suggestion to block four hours seemed impossible! But as I leaned into it, I realized that when you define your one thing, the impact is remarkable. Those four hours aren’t just time on a calendar—they’re a commitment to your priorities, your energy, and your vision. 

 

Now, the truth is not everyday do I block off four hours, due to the responsibilities of being a young dad, coaching clients, having a firm cut off time for work so I can be with family, etc. However, I AM making a gesture towards it and there IS a time block made in my planner each day for my one thing. 

 

What surprised me most was how much I was losing to small distractions:

  • Scrolling through Instagram.
  • Checking emails every 15 minutes.
  • Wandering off to grab water when I could have planned ahead.

These weren’t just “little” things—they were cracks in my focus that drained my energy and held me back from showing up fully as the man I want to be.

 

Many men struggle with this. We get caught up in the grind, pulled in a hundred directions, and wonder why we feel depleted. Concentration is a skill, and it’s one of the most important skills we can develop.

 

Time blocking has helped me reclaim my energy and channel it into what matters most. It’s not just about getting things done—it’s about becoming a more focused, present, and powerful man in all areas of life.

 

If this resonates with you, start small if needed, but start.

 

With focus and strength,
