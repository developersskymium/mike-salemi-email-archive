Pain is a signal, not the problem.

 

Most people think of pain as something to fix, suppress, or push through. But pain is simply the body’s way of telling you something is out of alignment. And whether it’s your back, knees, shoulders—or even your energy—the real cause often isn’t where you feel it.

 

Take back pain, for example. You might assume the issue is in your lower back, but in reality, the problem could be in your feet, hips, breathing patterns, or even your diet. If one area is out of sync, everything else has to compensate. That’s when movement becomes inefficient, tension builds up, and pain shows up.

 

Integrity, it's more than just a word.

 

This month inside The Vital Man Collective, our focus is integrity—and there are many ways to look at it. Three in particular shape how we show up in the world:

  • Word: Do you follow through on what you say, even to yourself? If you commit to something, do you honor it
  • Heart: Do you act in alignment with what you truly believe, or do you let fear, approval-seeking, or pressure pull you off course?
  • Body: Are you moving efficiently, or are old patterns of stress, misalignment, and imbalance leading to unnecessary pain and tension?

We recently had guest presenter Justin Brien, a certifying instructor for Guy Voyer and ELDOA, speak to the group about The Gravity Line—the principle that proper alignment allows for more efficient movement and reduces unnecessary strain.

 

When your head, ribs, pelvis, knees, and feet are stacked properly, movement becomes effortless. There’s no wasted energy, no unnecessary tension. But when you’re off-center? The body knows—and it lets you know through discomfort, fatigue, or injury.

 

It’s the same in life.

 

Ever notice how a tough conversation, financial stress, or an overwhelming to-do list can leave you feeling just as drained as physical pain? That’s because efficiency and integrity don’t just apply to movement—they apply to how you manage energy in every area of your life.

 

When you know how to dynamically center—physically, mentally, and energetically—you move through challenges with greater clarity and purpose. When you’re off-center, everything feels harder than it needs to be, and you get pulled into old, dysfunctional patterns.

 

This is where the gold is.

 

Can you develop enough awareness to know where your energetic center is moment to moment?

 

Do you have the sensitivity to discern which people, conversations, and tasks pull you out—causing a collapse of your power?

 

And most importantly, do you know how to bring yourself back?

 

This can be trained—and in my opinion, it should be trained, especially in men who seek to show up most powerfully and lovingly in life.
