Weekly Training Plan Recommendations:

 

WEDNESDAY (2/17): Full Workout

 

THURSDAY (2/18): Stretching Focus

Perform the stretches from yesterday demonstrated at the end of our session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to these stretches.

 

1. T8/T9 ELDOA

2. C4/C5 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

FRIDAY (2/19): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Focus on Kettlebell Exercise #1 for 1-3 sets, keeping the repetitions at 10 (each arm) or less. The focus here is maintaining proper positioning and control in the finishing position especially. The rest period will be as needed, and I suggest going one weight lighter if you have it compared to what you will use in tomorrow’s workout
  • You can also add in 1-2 sets of the Upper Abdominal Roll Ups with Rotations, for 8-10 reps each side following your Kettlebell Skill work
  • Finish with the T/8/T9 & L5/S1 ELDOAs for 1 minute each and rest well before your full workout tomorrow. 

 

 

SATURDAY (2/20): Full Workout

  • Perform the workout exactly as we did on Wednesday.
  • If you are able to progress on any of the exercises, explore so in the following ways:
  1. Add 1-4 repetitions to each main exercise (for Kettlebell Exercise #1, aim to accomplish more reps in the 20-30 second working time frame - depending on what time duration (20 or 30 seconds) you chose based on your level)
  2. Explore progressing to the next skill progression (based on where you finished on Wednesday)
  3. Add 1-2 sets 
  4. Add weight (if this is an option for you based on what you have access to at home)

 

 

SUNDAY (2/21): Rest and Play!

  • A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
  • Depending on your goals, as well as how you are feeling, I would suggest the following options to also consider:
  • Easy morning walk (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes 
  • Rowing Machine 1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time
  • Breathing Squats for 5-20 minutes.These can be found in Paul Chek’s book, How to Eat, Move, and Be Healthy. Or you can go to this link: https://www.paulcheksblog.com/how-to-work-in-with-breathing-squats/

 

 

MONDAY (2/22): Skill Development #2 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform 2-4 rounds of the Bulgarian Bag and Bodyweight Circuit. Aim to keep the rest periods in between sets between 30-45 seconds. 
  • Prior to your working sets, perform at least 1-2 technique sets where you focus on only one element you’d like to improve on for that particular exercise. The repetitions should be kept on the lower side for this technique work (approximately 4-8 reps)

Finish with these stretches as performed in the workout:

1. T8/T9 ELDOA

2. C4/C5 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 ELDOA
