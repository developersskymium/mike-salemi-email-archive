Here is Your Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (12/1): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (12/2): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

  • Bicep MFS Stretch x 45 sec x 2
  • T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

FRIDAY (12/3): Skill Development #1

  • Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform the following as one individual station:
    • Staggered Stance Clean & Press OR Staggered Stance Clean & Step Press OR Staggered Stance Clean & 2 Step Press x 5 reps x 2-4 sets, resting as needed (use a light weight. This is more for developing coordination and rhythm with the movement. You should not be sore from this for tomorrow's full training.)

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  • Bicep MFS Stretch x 45 sec x 2

  • T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min

  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

SATURDAY (12/4): Full Workout

 

SUNDAY (12/5): Recovery Day

  • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout

Finish with this cool down:

  • Bicep MFS Stretch x 45 sec x 2

  • T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min

  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

MONDAY (12/6): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

  • Perform the Following Supersets for 2-4 rounds, resting up to 60 sec:

​​​​​​​Superset #1

  1. Staggered Stance Clean & Press OR Staggered Stance Clean & Step Press OR Staggered Stance Clean & 2 Step Press x 8 reps each arm

  2. Bent Over Row with Reciprocity (towel optional) x 8-12 each arm

Superset #2

  1. Bulgarian Bag Swing Squat x 10

  2. Bulgarian Bag Drop and Catch x 20

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  • Bicep MFS Stretch x 45 sec x 2

  • T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min

  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

TUESDAY (12/7): Recovery Focus
Perform the cool down from the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:
