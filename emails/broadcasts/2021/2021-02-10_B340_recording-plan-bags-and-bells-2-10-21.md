Weekly Training Plan Recommendations:

THURSDAY (2/11): Stretching Focus

FRIDAY (2/12): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Focus on Exercise #1 & #2 in Circuit #1 from our training this week (for 2-3 sets)
For the Turkish Get Up, focus more on the bodyweight version for all levels today. Feel each position, and aim to create more space in your joints. Tomorrow, when you do the full workout, you can progress in any way you like that is mentioned (see tomorrow below). I would suggest if you’d like, you can increase the repetitions today by 1-3, and even go up to the full Get Up, however keep the load light or bodyweight so you’re not overly fatigued for tomorrow. 
For Exercise #2, reduce the reps in by 4-6 from what you did on Wednesday, and stick with 2-3 sets, resting as needed after the super set. Keep technique as the main focus and safe your energy for tomorrow. 
Finish with the Biceps Stretch (only 1 set of 45 seconds) & L5/S1 ELDOA for 1 minute and rest well before your full workout tomorrow. 

 

 

SATURDAY (2/13): Full Workout

Perform the workout exactly as we did on Wednesday. 
For the Spin Test specifically, aim to beat your time
If you are able to progress on the other exercises, explore so in the following ways:
Add 1-4 repetitions to each main exercise
Explore progressing to the next skill progression (based on where you finished on Wednesday)
For the Turkish Get Up:
Beginners - Progress by first adding a load, and focusing on only going up to the Elbow Post Position. Once you are comfortable with that, you can continue on to the High Hip
Intermediate - Progress to the full Turkish Get Up to the standing position if you are familiar with the addition steps. If not, stick, with the Half Get Up, and increase any of the progressions mentioned in this list
Add 1-2 sets to one or both circuits, and/or the Bodyweight Wall Sit Squat
Add weight (if this is an option for you based on what you have access to at home)

 

 

SUNDAY (2/14): Rest and Play!

A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
Depending on your goals, as well as how you are feeling, I would suggest the following options to also consider:
Hiking for ~45 minutes (long duration cardio, moderate walking pace)
Rowing Machine ~8-15 minutes (cardio focus, moderate pace)
Meditation 5-20 minutes (especially if you are feeling physically or mentally stressed)

 

 

MONDAY (2/15): Skill Development #2 and Stretching

Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Perform your Spin Test only from Wednesday’s workout (aim to beat your time)
If you would like, following your Spin Test, you can also perform 1-3 sets of the Static Wall Sit Squats for 1 min (remember to lower down out of the squat when reaching your threshold).

Biceps Stretch
