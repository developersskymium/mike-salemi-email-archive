FRIDAY (10/1): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

Perform the below, with the mouth fully closed throughout
Rotational Press (Light) x 8 x 1-3
Rotational Clean (light) x 8 x 1-3
Ab Roll Ups x 10 x 1-3

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

Levator Scapula Stretch x 1 min each
C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

SATURDAY (10/2): Full Workout

Perform the workout as on Wednesday
You can increase the rounds up to 6 for the rotational kettlebell work, and do your best to beat your time during the spin test. 

 

SUNDAY (10/3):

Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout

Finish with this cool down:

Levator Scapula Stretch x 1 min each
C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

MONDAY (10/4): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

Today will be fast and challenging. What I'd like you to do is 1 round sets of 10/10 Bulgarian Bag Spins + 10 Push Ups. After each round, I'd like you to rest up to 60 seconds, and repeat for a total of 4-6 times. Each set however, your goal is to move as fast as possible and focus on technique and acceleration.
Following this, perform Single Arm Bent Over Kettebell Rows with a parallel stance x 10 reps each side x 3 sets

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

Levator Scapula Stretch x 1 min each
C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min
