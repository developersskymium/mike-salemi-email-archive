# Leg Training with the Bulgarian Bag (infographic)

Hi {first_name},

⁠⠀

While there's countless movements that you can do with the Bulgarian Bag, this one in particular, the Swing Squat, is among the most effective for training legs.⁠⠀

⁠⠀

What you'll experience when done correctly is a deep conditioning of the legs (primarily the quads, hamstrings, and glutes) and back, as well as improved coordination. ⁠

⁠⠀

Where I see most people experiencing challenge with this movement is on coordinating the lower and upper body.⁠⠀

⁠⠀

Many times when first learning, people will perform it like a classic swing (just as you would a kettlebell), however, for a proper swing squat, that would be incorrect. With this movement, the goal is when the Bulgarian Bag raises, the hips drop, and visa-versa. ⁠⠀
