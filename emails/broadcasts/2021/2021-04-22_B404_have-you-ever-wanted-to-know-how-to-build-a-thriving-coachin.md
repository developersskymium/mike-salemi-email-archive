# Have you ever wanted to know how to build a thriving coaching business?

Hi {first_name},

 

Last week, I was having dinner with a close friend - a high-level, talented practitioner. He's someone who is incredible with movement, bodywork, nutrition & lifestyle coaching, and more.

 

He works with many clients (from moms, to the elderly, all the way up to professional athletes we see on TV) and the results he gets are LEGIT.

 

There's one interesting thing, however, that I've noticed in him, in many of my other friends, and even myself until about 3 years ago....

 

His approach to business could more accurately be described as a passionate "hobby" that generates some income, rather than a sustainable, thriving business. 

 

For me personally, for the first ~12 years as a trainer, I didn't want to acknowledge that business itself was a huge weak link for me.

 

I also refused to look at things like:

My self-limiting beliefs around money
My imposture syndrome
The shame and embarrassment I felt when on putting myself out on social media and promoting what I did
How petrified I was ask for a sale

You see, I identified a coach and teacher but certainly not a businessman. 

 

In over 15 years, I have learned a lot of lessons along the way, and am continually learning more each day about how to build a thriving, healthy, holistic business.
