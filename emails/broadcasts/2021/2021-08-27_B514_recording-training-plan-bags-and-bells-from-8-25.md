See if you can maintain a consistent number of breaths, as well as the duration of each breath within every rep of each progression. Also, stay nasal breathing throughout.

KB Two Hand Swing x 10-15 x 1
KB Single Arm Swing x 10-15 ea x 1

L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

SATURDAY (8/28): Full Workout

SUNDAY (8/29): Active Rest (OR Complete Off Day)
