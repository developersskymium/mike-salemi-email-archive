Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (6/23): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (6/24): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

  1. Walk It Out 
  2. Sky to Toe Reaches
  3. Stretches (Groin, Quads, Hamstrings, Rhomboids) and Floor Flow

 

FRIDAY (6/25): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform the following circuit (2-4 rounds, 20 sec on, 20 sec rest):
  1. ​​Spin x 20sec Left (20s REST)
  2. ​Spin x 20sec Right (20s REST)
  3. Kettlebell Swing OR Swing-Swing Squat Combo x 20sec (20s REST)
  • Once you've completed the above, rest for 1-2 minutes and then perform 1-3 sets of Single Arm Kettlebell Rows for 10 sec Left Arm + 10 sec Right Arm with 20 second REST

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

  1. Walk It Out 
  2. Sky to Toe Reaches
  3. Stretches (Groin, Quads, Hamstrings, Rhomboids) and Floor Flow

 

SATURDAY (6/26): Full Workout

  • Perform the workout as on Wednesday 

 

SUNDAY (6/27): Rest and Play! 

  • Cycling Sprints (if you've got extra juice ; ): Cycle easy for 5-10 minutes to start. Then once warm, Sprint for 5-10 sec > Cycle Easy Recovery for 50 sec and repeat this superset 6-10x. Complete this session with a 5-10 minute easy cycling cool down. 
  • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout
  • Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
  • Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down:

  1. Walk It Out 
  2. Sky to Toe Reaches
  3. Stretches (Groin, Quads, Hamstrings, Rhomboids) and Floor Flow

 

MONDAY (6/28): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

  • Perform the following circuits as laid out below:
  • CIRCUIT 1 (2-4 rounds, 30 sec on, 15 sec rest)
    1. ​Alternating Arm Throw or Shoulder Load x 30sec (15s REST)
    2. Bulgarian Bag Hammer Snatch x 30sec (15s REST)

REST 1-3 Minutes following Circuit 1 before starting Circuit 2

  • CIRCUT 2 (2-4 rounds, 20 sec on, 10 sec rest)
  1. Kettlebell Lunge and Pass x 30sec (15s REST)
  2. Push Ups x 30sec (15s REST)
  3. Kettlebell Rows: 30sec (15s REST)

Finish with this cool down:

  1. Walk It Out 
  2. Sky to Toe Reaches
  3. Stretches (Groin, Quads, Hamstrings, Rhomboids) and Floor Flow
