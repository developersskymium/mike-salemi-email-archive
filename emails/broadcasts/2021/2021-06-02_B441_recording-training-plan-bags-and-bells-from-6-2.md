Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (6/2): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (6/3): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

Feet Up the Wall:  for this one (we did not demo this specifically), simply lay for 2-5+ minutes with your back flat on the floor and your feet resting up the wall. You'll want to straighten your legs just to the point that you feel a gentle stretch behind the thighs. From here simply breathe deeply. This is a regression to the L5/S1 ELDOA and a part of the starting place for it. This gentle stretch is highly beneficial to go for longer durations at very light effort.
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 minute

Explore the below options depending on your goals and try to remain nasal-diaphragmatic (belly) breathing
