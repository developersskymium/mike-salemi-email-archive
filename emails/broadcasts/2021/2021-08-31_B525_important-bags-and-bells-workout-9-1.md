Here is Your Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (9/1): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (9/2): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

Bicep Stretch x 45 sec x 2 sets
Outside Hamstring Stretch x 45 sec x 2 sets each side
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

FRIDAY (9/3): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Perform the below Super Set Circuit, resting as needed in between. Do your best to stay nasal breathing throughout. 
Bulgarian Bag Clean x 10-12 x 3-4
Bulgarian Bag Half Spin OR Half Spin to Alt. Curtsy Squat x 6 ea x 3-4

Once You Complete the Above Skill Work, Perform the Following Super Sets:

Body Weight Half Windmill to Elbow OR Hand (Slow and Controlled Tempo): x 6-10 x 2-3
Abdominal Roll Ups (straight up and down the middle) x 8-10 x 2-3

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

Bicep Stretch x 45 sec x 2 sets
Outside Hamstring Stretch x 45 sec x 2 sets each side
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

SATURDAY (9/4): Full Workout

Perform the workout as on Wednesday

 

SUNDAY (9/5): Active Rest (OR Complete Off/Play Day)

Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout

Finish with this cool down:

Bicep Stretch x 45 sec x 2 sets
Outside Hamstring Stretch x 45 sec x 2 sets each side
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

MONDAY (9/6): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

Perform the following exercises as individual stations (not a circuit):

 

Bulgarian Bag Clean + Squat x 10 x 3 sets [ rest up to 30 sec ] 
Half Kneeling Windmill OR Windmill (to elbow or hand) + Press Combo x 10 or 5/5 for Combos x 3 sets [ rest up to 30 sec ] 
Bulgarian Bag Alt. Curtsy Squat OR Half Spin to Curtsy Combo x 8 ea x 3 sets  [ rest up to 60 sec ] 
Staggered Stance Row x 8-10 ea [ rest up to 60 sec ] 

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

Bicep Stretch x 45 sec x 2 sets
Outside Hamstring Stretch x 45 sec x 2 sets each side
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

TUESDAY (9/7): Recovery Focus
Perform the cool down from the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.
