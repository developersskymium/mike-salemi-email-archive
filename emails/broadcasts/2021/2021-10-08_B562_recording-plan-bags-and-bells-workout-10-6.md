Here is Your Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (10/6): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (10/7): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

Psoas Stretch x 45 sec x 2 ea
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

FRIDAY (10/8): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Perform the below superset, with the mouth fully closed throughout, resting as needed in between 
BB 1/2 Spin OR Spin with Half Step Over x 6 each side x 1-3
Take as many warm up sets and reps needed prior to the above BB exercise. Progressive warm ups include: Open Arm Side to Side Swing, Open Arm Side to Side Swing with Counter Balance, and Open Arm Side to Side Swing with Rotation
Knee Over Toe Lunges x 8 each side x 1-3 

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

Psoas Stretch x 45 sec x 2 ea
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

SATURDAY (10/9): Full Workout

Perform the workout as on Wednesday

 

SUNDAY (10/10):

Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout

Finish with this cool down:

Psoas Stretch x 45 sec x 2 ea
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

MONDAY (10/11): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

Perform the Following Two Supersets:
SUPER SET 1: 3-5 Sets (Rest up to 45 sec)
 BB 1/2 Spin OR Spin with Half Step Over x 8 ea.
KB Open Palm Grip Bent Over Row x 10-15 
SUPER SET 2: 3-5 Sets (No Rest)
Half Kneeling KB Overhead Press OR Straddle Overhead Press x 6-10 ea
Bodyweight or KB Knee Over Toe Lunges x 6-10 ea.

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

Psoas Stretch x 45 sec x 2 ea
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

TUESDAY (10/12): Recovery Focus
Perform the cool down from the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

Psoas Stretch x 45 sec x 2 ea
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min
