Here is Your Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (11/10): Snatch Test, Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (11/11): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

  • L4/L5 ELDOA x 1 min
  • C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

FRIDAY (11/12): Snatch Workout #2

  • Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform the following Kettlebell Snatch Sets
    • 1 minute on each arm, 1 minute off x 6-10 sets (keep a pace that is fast yet able to maintain or even increase as you progress through the sets. As a reference, I would suggest no less than 15 reps in one minute, and no more than 22. 
    • NOTE: For a level I regression, perform single arm swings instead of snatches, and do 6-10 sets of 30 seconds on, 45 seconds off
  • Following your Snatch / Swing sets above, focus on the below exercises to develop general strength in any manner that you'd like - either station training, or in a circuit for more of a conditioning effect. The rest duration will be up to you, however keep it below 90 seconds or as soon as you feel fully fresh.
    1. KB Knee Over Toe Lunges x 8-12 each 
    2. Single or Double Kettlebell Bent Over Row x 10 ea
    3. Abdominal Roll Ups x 8-20

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  • L4/L5 ELDOA x 1 min
  • C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

SATURDAY (11/13): Recovery Day

Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout

Finish with this cool down:

  • L4/L5 ELDOA x 1 min
  • C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

SUNDAY (11/14): Snatch Training #3

  • Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform the following Kettlebell Snatch Sets
    • 2 minutes each arm on, 2 minutes off x 3 sets (reduce the weight or pace from your last session by ~4 reps. Take your time with this and utilize the overhead lockout position as a time for rest. 

 

NOTE: For a level I regression, perform single arm swings instead of snatches, and do 4-6 sets of 60 seconds on each arm, 90 seconds off

 

Following your Snatch / Swing sets above, perform the following circuit for 2-4 rounds, and a rest period of up to 60 seconds:

  1. Bulgarian Bag Spins OR Half Spins x 8-12 each 
  2. Bulgarian Bag Bent Over Rows x 15
  3. Push Ups x 8-12

 

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  • L4/L5 ELDOA x 1 min
  • C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

 

MONDAY (11/15): Recovery Focus
Perform the cool down from the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

  • L4/L5 ELDOA x 1 min
  • C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

TUESDAY (11/16): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

  • Perform the Following Circuit for 1-3 rounds, resting up to 90 sec:
    1. Kettlebell Two Hand Swings x 8-10
    2. Bulgarian Bag Spins OR Half Spins x 8-10 ea
    3. Kettlebell Bent Over Row with Reciprocity x 8-12 ea

 

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  • L4/L5 ELDOA x 1 min
  • C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min
