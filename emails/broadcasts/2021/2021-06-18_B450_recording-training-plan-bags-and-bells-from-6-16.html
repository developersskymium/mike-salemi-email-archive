Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (6/16): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (6/17): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

  1. Biceps Myofascial Stretch x 45 sec x 2
  2. T8/T9 ELDOA (level I) OR T7/T8 ELDOA (level II) x 1 min
  3. C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
  4. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

FRIDAY (6/18): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Today's focus is gong to be on the "Whip" element during your Bulgarian Bag Spin. The whip technique happens as the Bag accelerates past your hip and into the finish position behind the back at shoulder blade level. When you do this your goal is to finish behind the back with the handles pointing upwards. 
  • Perform the below in a Superset fashion (back to back) with up to 30 second rest
  1. Half Spin OR Full Spin with a Pause: 3-6 sets x 8-12 reps each side
  2. Kettlebell Bent Two Hand Over Row with or without Rotation: 3-6 sets x 12-16 reps

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

  1. Biceps Myofascial Stretch x 45 sec x 2
  2. T8/T9 ELDOA (level I) OR T7/T8 ELDOA (level II) x 1 min
  3. C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
  4. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

SATURDAY (6/19): Full Workout

  • Perform the workout as on Wednesday. 
  • **Remember to do your best to stay only breathing in and out through the nose when you do this workout. Your pacing will be determine by your ability to remain calm and continue breathing through the nose. 

 

 

SUNDAY (6/20): Rest and Play! 

  • A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
  • Explore the below options depending on your goals and try to remain nasal-diaphragmatic (belly) breathing
  • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes 
  • Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
  • Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down:

  1. Biceps Myofascial Stretch x 45 sec x 2
  2. T8/T9 ELDOA (level I) OR T7/T8 ELDOA (level II) x 1 min
  3. C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
  4. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

 

MONDAY (6/21): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

 

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start 
  • Perform the below in a circuit fashion (back to back) with up to 60 second rest ((aim to maintain breathing only in and out through the nose))
  1. Half Spin OR Full Spin with a Pause: 4-6 sets x 8-15 reps each side
  2. Swing Squat: 4-6 sets x 8-12 reps
  3. Bulgarian Bag Sit Up and Press OR to **Stand and Press: 4-6 sets x 6-10 reps

*This exercise we did not demo however is a progression from the Bulgarian Bag Sit Up to Press you are familiar with. If you have the ability, as you roll your body up, use controlled momentum to rise up onto your feet and stand. Once in the standing position, press overhead and repeat the movement. You will love this one!

  • Finish with this cool down:
  1. Biceps Myofascial Stretch x 45 sec x 2
  2. T8/T9 ELDOA (level I) OR T7/T8 ELDOA (level II) x 1 min
  3. C6/C7 ELDOA x 1 min
  4. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min
