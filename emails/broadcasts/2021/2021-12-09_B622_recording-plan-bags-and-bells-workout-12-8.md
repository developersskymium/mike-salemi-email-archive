# [Recording + Plan] Bags and Bells Workout 12/8

Hi {first_name},

 

Here is your link to the workout* from 12/8: 

https://vimeo.com/654724047/8144882d25

*This is also available in your Mighty Networks now

 

Here is Your Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (12/8): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (12/9): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

General ELDOA x 5/5

 

FRIDAY (12/10): Skill Development #1

Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Perform the following as one individual station:
Spin OR Arm Throw x 20 seconds on x 20 seconds off x 4-8 sets
With this, I'd like you to pick one element within this movement and hone in on only one component and drill that in. In our last class for example, on the spin, we really dialed in the lowering phase, and keeping the lead arm close to the shoulder by pulling the elbow back. If this is your one weakpoint, that is the only thing that you'd focus on
Bent Over KB Row x 10-15 each arm

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

General ELDOA x 5/5

 

SATURDAY (12/11): Full Workout

 

 

SUNDAY (12/12): Recovery Day

Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout

Finish with this cool down:

General ELDOA x 5/5

 

MONDAY (12/13): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

3 Exercises, done in station (singular exercise, no circuit) format

Kettlebell Swings x 10 reps x 3-6 sets, with 30 seconds rest

Spin (either open palm grip or close palmed, inside grip) for 1 rep with a transitional element to the other side. For this, you would perform one swing and one transition to the other side. Here are a few ways to transition that we've covered in the past:

Spin-Pummel

Spin-Side Swing with Rotation

Spin-Low Side Swing (no rotation)

Spin-Side Swing Squat

Spin-Shoulder Load

Spin-Arm Throw

For this type of training, what I'd like you do do is:

1 Spin Left + Transition, 1 Spin Right + Transition, 2 Spin Left + Transition, 3 Spin Right + Transiiton, 3 Spin Left +Transition, 3 Spin Right + Transiiton

If you have a lower conditioning level, or are a level 1 participant, I want you to stop at 3 reps each side. If you have a higher conditioning level, I'd like you to go up to 5 reps.

Take as much rest as needed, up to 7 minutes, after one sequence, and perform up to 3 rounds of this

KB Bent Over Row with Reciprocity using a Towel (for grip) x 8-12 each arm

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

General ELDOA x 5/5

 

TUESDAY (12/14): Recovery Focus
Perform the cool down from the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

General ELDOA x 5/5

 

Enjoy!
