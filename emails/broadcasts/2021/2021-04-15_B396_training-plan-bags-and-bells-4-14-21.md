Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (4/14): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (4/15): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

 

1. Quad (Rectus Femoris) Myofascial Stretch

2. T6/T7 ELDOA - perform (2) sets - (1) in each lower body position

3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

FRIDAY (4/16): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Once you complete your warm up, perform one set as demonstrated in the workout of the Royal Coil. Hold each side for 5 seconds. 
Perform 1-3 sets of the following exercises for 8-10 reps each side, resting as needed in between. This is not an EMOM, Take your time on each exercise and focus on the precision of each rep as well as the side bending as we went over in the class. Aim to be as powerful as possible on every rep. 
Kettlebell Coiling Swing OR Classic Two Hand Swing
Kettlebell RMT Pivot Press OR Classic Single Arm Overhead Press

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Quad (Rectus Femoris) Myofascial Stretch

2. T6/T7 ELDOA - perform (2) sets - (1) in each lower body position

3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

SATURDAY (4/17): Full Workout

Perform the workout exactly as we did on Wednesday. 
Perform (1) additional complete round of training so it is (4) rounds total
The load is up to you if you would like to change the kettlebell/bag weight if you feel confident

 

SUNDAY (4/18): Rest and Play! 

A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
Depending on your goals, as well as how you are feeling, I would suggest the following options to also consider:
Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes 
Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
Rowing Machine 500 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time
Sprints of 20 yards x 3-8 sets, resting as needed (if you do this, perform the core temperature warm up and joint mobility beforehand)

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Quad (Rectus Femoris) Myofascial Stretch

2. T6/T7 ELDOA - perform (2) sets - (1) in each lower body position

3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

MONDAY (4/19): Skill Development #2 and Stretching

Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Once you complete your warm up, perform one priming set as demonstrated in the workout of the Royal Coil. Hold each side for 5 seconds. 
Perform 2-4 circuit of the all four exercises listed below for 6-8 reps each side, resting as needed in between. While this is not an EMOM, you can take up to 45 seconds rest after each exercise. Because your rest period has increased, if you feel confident in increasing the load you can go for it.  
Kettlebell Coiling Swing OR Classic Two Hand Swing
Kettlebell RMT Pivot Press OR Classic Single Arm Overhead Press
Bulgarian Bag Half Spin Squat Jump OR Half Swing and Squat
Four Point Single Arm Body Row OR Four Point Hold (30 sec)

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Quad (Rectus Femoris) Myofascial Stretch

2. T6/T7 ELDOA - perform (2) sets - (1) in each lower body position

3. L5/S1 ELDOA
