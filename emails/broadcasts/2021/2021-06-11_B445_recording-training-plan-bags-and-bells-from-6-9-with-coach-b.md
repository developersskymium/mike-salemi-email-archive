FRIDAY (6/11): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Today we will focus on KB Snatch or Swing training and technqiue. Once you warm and work up to your desired level of progression/difficulty below, then from there complete 10-15 reps x 4-6 sets at full recovery (as much time as you need for you to feel totally back to normal - don't take more than 4  minutes though). Once you complete these sets perform 3-4 sets of 10 reps of Single Arm Bent Over Rows.  
Two Hand Deadlift
Two Hand Swing 
Single Arm Swing
Single Arm Snatch

Walk It Out 
Sky to Toe Reaches
Stretches (Quads and Hamstrings)
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 minute

 

SATURDAY (6/12): Full Workout

Perform the workout as on Wednesday 

 

 

SUNDAY (6/13): Rest and Play! 

A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
Explore the below options depending on your goals and try to remain nasal-diaphragmatic (belly) breathing
Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes 
Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down:

Walk It Out 
Sky to Toe Reaches
Upper Body Stretches (Quads and Hamstrings)
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 minute

 

 

MONDAY (6/14): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

 

Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Perform a Superset (back to back sets of the below two exercises):
Spins 
Kettlebell Swings or Snatches
When you do the above superset, your goal is to complete a total of 80 each.  After each super set, you can rest up to 1 minute before your next combo set. Here's an example of what your first 3 sets might look like:
Set 1: Spins (15/15) + Snatches (10/10) -- REST 30 sec
Set 2: Spins (15/15) + Snatches (10/10) -- REST 30 sec
Set 3 Spins (8/8) + Snatches (10/10) -- REST 30 sec
Continue the above until your total work is 80 Spins and 80 Snatches
Finish with this cool down:
Walk It Out 
Sky to Toe Reaches
Stretches (Quads and Hamstrings)
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 minute
