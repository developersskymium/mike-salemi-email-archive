Weekly Training Plan Recommendations:

 

WEDNESDAY (1/27): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (1/28): Stretching Focus

Perform the stretches from yesterday demonstrated at the end of our session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to these stretches:

 

1. *Glute Max Deep Myofascial Stretch

2. Hip ELDOA (External Rotation)

3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

*Add one additional set to the lat stretch, and perform 3 sets of 30 seconds with a 10 second rest in between on each side.

 

 

FRIDAY (1/29): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Pick either Exercise #1 from Circuit #1 OR Exercise #1 from Circuit #1 that you found challenging or that you’d like to work on.
  • Perform 2-3 sets of this exercise for 6-10 reps each side utilizing a lighter load and resting as needed in between. Focus on skill improve and technique during these sets. 
  • Finish with the L5/S1 ELDOA for 1 minute and rest well before your full workout tomorrow. 

 

SATURDAY (1/30): Full Workout

Perform the workout exactly as we did on Wednesday. 

If you are able to progress, explore so in the following ways:

 

  1. Add 1-4 repetitions to each exercise
  2. Explore progressing to the next skill progression (based on where you finished on Wednesday)
  3. Add 1-2 sets to one or both circuits
  4. Add weight (if this is an option for you based on what you have access to at home)

 

SUNDAY (1/31): Rest and Play!

A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 

If you wish to turn this into more of a training session, continuous steady-state cardio is recommend. Getting in a pool or hiking are an excellent options as example. Keep the total time you are in the pool up to 20 minutes, and hiking between 15 minutes to 1 hour. Have the pace be steady and controlled, resting as needed.

 

Enjoy this day above all and let your energy and how your body feels guide you to the best of your ability. If you do decide to perform cardio, I highly suggest doing your Core Temperature Warm Up and Bodyweight Mobility series from the workout prior to cardio. Once you complete your session, at minimum perform your L5/S1 ELDOA (1 minute), and depending on the time available to you, you can perform your Glute Stretch (3 sets of 30 seconds with 10 second rest in between sides) as well as your Hip ELODA (1 minute each side).

 

 

MONDAY (2/1): Skill Development #2 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform Exercise #1 from Circuit #1 AND Exercise #1 from Circuit #2
  • Do 1-2 sets of each of these two exercises for 6-10 reps utilizing a light load and resting as needed in between. Focus on skill improvement and technique above all on these sets
  • Once you have done this focused skill work, select either Circuit #1 AND Circuit #2 and perform 1-3 rounds of a complete circuit. 

Finish with these three stretches as performed in the workout:

1. *Glute Max Deep Myofascial Stretch

2. Hip ELDOA (External Rotation)

3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

*Add one additional set to the lat stretch, and perform 3 sets of 30 seconds with a 10 second rest in between on each side.
