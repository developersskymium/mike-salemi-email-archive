Weekly Training Plan Recommendations:

 

WEDNESDAY (1/20): Full Workout

 

THURSDAY (1/21): Stretching Focus

  • Perform the stretches from yesterday demonstrated at the end of our session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to these stretches.
  1. *Lat Stretch
  2. Shoulder ELDOA
  3. L5/S1 ELDOA

*Add one additional set to the lat stretch, and perform 3 sets of 30 seconds with a 10 second rest in between on each side.

 

FRIDAY (1/22): Skill Development and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Pick the one skill from Circuit #1 OR Circuit #2 that you found challenging or that you’d like to work on.
  • Perform 1-3 sets of this exercise for 6-10 reps utilizing a light load and resting as needed in between. 
  • Finish with the L5/S1 ELDOA and rest well before your full workout tomorrow. 

SATURDAY (1/23): Full Workout

  • Perform the workout exactly as we did on Wednesday. 
  • If you are able to progress, explore so in the following ways:
  1. Add 1-4 repetitions to each exercise
  2. Explore progressing to the next skill progression (based on where you finished on Wednesday)
  3.  Add 1-2 sets to one or both circuits
  4. Add weight (if this is an option for you based on what you have access to at home)

SUNDAY (1/24): Rest and Play!

 

MONDAY (1/25): Skill Development, Circuit #1, and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Pick the one skill from both Circuit #1 AND Circuit #2 that you found challenging or that you’d like to work on. 
  • Perform 1-3 sets of each of these two exercises for 6-10 reps utilizing a light load and resting as needed in between. 
  • Once you have done this focused skill work, select either Circuit #1 OR Circuit #2 and perform 1-3 rounds. 

Finish with these three stretches as performed in the workout:

  1. *Lat Stretch
  2. Shoulder ELDOA
  3. L5/S1 ELDOA

*Add one additional set to the lat stretch, and perform 3 sets of 30 seconds with a 10 second rest in between on each side.

 

TUESDAY (1/26): Stretching Focus

 

  • Perform the stretches from yesterday demonstrated at the end of our session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to these stretches.
  1. *Lat Stretch
  2. Shoulder ELDOA
  3. L5/S1 ELDOA

*Add one additional set to the lat stretch, and perform 3 sets of 30 seconds with a 10 second rest in between on each side.
