While bands are a hidden gem IMO in fitness, it certainly helps to know optimal ways for how to use it to improve movement awareness, skill, and play.

 

Click here for a quick video I created for you demonstrating and breaking down the Pummel to Rotational Lunge.

 

Movement Technique Steps/Tips:

Pull the loaded arm to the centerline of the chest
Pivot and rotate the body 90 deg.
Circle the elbow 
Once you complete the rotation, descend under control
Extend the free arm to aid in rotation
Reverse the steps about and return to the start position
