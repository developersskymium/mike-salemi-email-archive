Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (7/21): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (7/22): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

  1. Bicep Myofascial Stretch: 3 sets of 30 seconds with 10 second rest
  2. T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set
  3. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

FRIDAY (7/23): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform the following superset for 2-6 rounds, resting as needed in between sets (up to 60 sec):
  1. Bulgarian Bag Half Spin OR Spin with Pause OR Half Spin to Pivot Lunge x 6-10 each side
  2. Side Lying Hip Raises x 15-25 

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  1. Bicep Myofascial Stretch: 3 sets of 30 seconds with 10 second rest
  2. T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set
  3. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

SATURDAY (7/24): Full Workout

  • Perform the workout as on Wednesday 

 

SUNDAY (7/25): Rest and Play! 

  • Cycling Sprints - if you've got the energy and want to push it. Cycle easy for 5-10 minutes to start. Then once warm, Sprint for 5-10 sec > Cycle Easy Recovery for 50 sec and repeat this superset 6-10x. Complete this session with a 5-10 minute easy cycling cool down. 
  • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout
  • Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
  • Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down:

  1. Bicep Myofascial Stretch: 3 sets of 30 seconds with 10 second rest
  2. T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set
  3. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

MONDAY (7/26): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

Circuit: Perform 2-4 rounds of the four exercises below back-to-back, resting up to 90 seconds after each circuit:

  • Bulgarian Bag Half Spin OR Spin with Pause OR Half Spin to Pivot Lunge x 8-5 each side
  • Side Lying Hip Raises x 15-30 
  • Floor Press OR Bridge Press OR Single Leg Bridge Press x 8-12

*Note, before heading to the next exercise, if needed, take up to 10-15 seconds recovery for the legs

  • Racked Position Front Squat (All levels): x 10-12 (switch arms at rep 5-6)

Finish with this cool down:

  1. Bicep Myofascial Stretch: 3 sets of 30 seconds with 10 second rest
  2. T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set
  3. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.
