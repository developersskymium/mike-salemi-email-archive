Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (3/24): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (3/25): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

1. Walk It Out

2. Sky to Toe Reaches

3. Muscle Group Stretches (Hamstrings, Quads, and Upper Body)

 

 

FRIDAY (3/26): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform 1 set of each of the exercises that you will perform in the full workout tomorrow. Rest as needed and treat this is a recovery/preparation type session. I suggest keeping the reps between 8-10 on each or below.
  1. Spins Alternating
  2. Push Up to KB Pull Through
  3. Snatch
  4. Side Handle Single Leg Alternating RDL to Reverse Lunge
  5. KB Goblet Squat with Front Kick
  6. Bulgarian Bag Mtn Climbers (wide around the bag)
  7. Squat and Press

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Walk It Out

2. Sky to Toe Reaches

3. Muscle Group Stretches (Hamstrings, Quads, and Upper Body)

 

 

SATURDAY (3/27): Full Workout

Perform the workout exactly as we did on Wednesday.

 

 

SUNDAY (3/28): Rest and Play!

  • A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
  • Depending on your goals, as well as how you are feeling, I would suggest the following options to also consider:
  • Easy morning walk (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes 
  • Rowing Machine 1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time
  • Sprints of 20 yards x 6-10 sets, resting as needed (if you do this, perform the core temperature warm up and joint mobility beforehand)

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Walk It Out

2. Sky to Toe Reaches

3. Muscle Group Stretches (Hamstrings, Quads, and Upper Body)

 

 

MONDAY (3/29): Skill Development #2 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform Tabata Supersets Below:
  • 20 seconds on & 10 seconds Rest-  8 rounds (4 rounds of each)
  • Snatch > Side Handle Single Leg Alternating RDL to Reverse Lunge

Rest 2-3 mins

 

Perform Tabata Supersets Below:

  • 20 seconds on & 10 seconds Rest-  8 rounds (4 rounds of each)
  • KB Goblet Squat with Front Kick > Bulgarian Bag Mtn Climbers (wide around the bag)

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Walk It Out

2. Sky to Toe Reaches

3. Muscle Group Stretches (Hamstrings, Quads, and Upper Body)
