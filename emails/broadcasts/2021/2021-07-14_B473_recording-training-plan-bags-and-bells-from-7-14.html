Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (7/14): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (7/15): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

  1. Rib 10 ELDOA x 1 min total each side (in the beginning, either base with your bottom hand, or split up into two sets if you need to keep the quality high)
  2. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

FRIDAY (7/16): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform the following superset for 2-4 rounds, resting as needed in between sets:
  1. Forward Step Swing OR Forward Step Half Spin OR Forward Walking Spin x 6-12 reps each side. 
  2. Ab Roll Ups x 8-12 reps

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  1. Rib 10 ELDOA x 1 min total each side (in the beginning, either base with your bottom hand, or split up into two sets if you need to keep the quality high)
  2. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

SATURDAY (7/117): Full Workout

  • Perform the workout as on Wednesday 

 

SUNDAY (7/18): Rest and Play! 

  • Cycling Sprints if you've got juice in the tank and want to push it. Cycle easy for 5-10 minutes to start. Then once warm, Sprint for 5-10 sec > Cycle Easy Recovery for 50 sec and repeat this superset 6-10x. Complete this session with a 5-10 minute easy cycling cool down. 
  • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout
  • Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
  • Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down:

  1. Rib 10 ELDOA x 1 min total each side (in the beginning, either base with your bottom hand, or split up into two sets if you need to keep the quality high)
  2. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

MONDAY (7/19): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

  • Perform the following first exercise as a standalone, followed by the circuit afterwards as laid
  1. Forward Step Swing OR Forward Step Half Spin OR Forward Walking Spin x 8-16 reps each side x 3-8 sets (your walk back to the starting position will be your active rest)

Circuit: Perform 1-4 rounds, resting up to 30 seconds after each circuit

  1. Bulgarian Split Squat OR Goblet KB Bulgarian Split Squat OR KB Racked Thruster in a Bulgarian Split Squat Positon (Tempo is 5 seconds lowering down, 1-2 second hold at the bottom, and explosive on the way up)
  2. Reptilian Crawing x Max Technique
  3. Butterflies x 10-30 reps Max Technique (this one was not in the workout from Wednesday, however we've done it quite a bit in the past and if you have Kettlebell Lifestyle it is in there too. This is where you lay on your belly, arm in a Y position. With your chest pressed into the ground and forehead resting on the floor, stickily raise your arm up as you bring your shoulder blades together)

Finish with this cool down:

  1. Rib 10 ELDOA x 1 min total each side (in the beginning, either base with your bottom hand, or split up into two sets if you need to keep the quality high)
  2. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set
