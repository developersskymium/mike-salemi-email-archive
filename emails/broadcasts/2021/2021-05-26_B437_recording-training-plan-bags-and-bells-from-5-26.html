Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (5/26): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (5/27): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

  1. Forearm Myofascial Stretches (3 Position: Fingers Back, at a 45 Degree Angle, and Turned in As Much As Possible)
  2. Shoulder ELDOA (2 Position: Fingers Up and Back)
  3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

FRIDAY (5/28): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform Any Of the Following Exercise Progressions for 6-10 reps each (this can also be used as a technical warm up progression before your training sets)
  1. Two Hand Deadlift
  2. Two Hand Swing
  3. Single Arm Swing
  4. Single Arm Swing Floating High Pull
  5. Alternating Floating Hight Pull x 1 plus Half Snatch x 1
  • When you are ready to start, perform 1-4 rounds of 6-10 reps of this exercise followed by bodyweight Get Ups for 8-10 reps
  • Rest period is up to you, since tomorrow will be a full workout, take additional rest here after each round (up to 1 min) so you are fully recovered

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

  1. Forearm Myofascial Stretches (3 Position: Fingers Back, at a 45 Degree Angle, and Turned in As Much As Possible)
  2. Shoulder ELDOA (2 Position: Fingers Up and Back)
  3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

SATURDAY (5/29): Full Workout

  • Perform the workout as on Wednesday. Feel free to add to the below if you'd like to add additional volume:
  •  1-2 additional rounds of the Bulgarian Bag Circuit

 

 

SUNDAY (5/30): Rest and Play! 

  • A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
  • Explore the below options depending on your goals and try to remain nasal-diaphragmatic (belly) breathing
  • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes 
  • Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
  • Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

  1. Forearm Myofascial Stretches (3 Position: Fingers Back, at a 45 Degree Angle, and Turned in As Much As Possible)
  2. Shoulder ELDOA (2 Position: Fingers Up and Back)
  3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

MONDAY (5/31): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

 

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform 1-6 rounds of 6-10 reps of the following exercises, taking 30-60 seconds rest following each one:
    1. ​Alternating Pummel OR Alternating Spin-Pummel Combo
    2. Pause Squat OR Pause Squat Thruster with Alternating Rotation
    3. Alternating Shoulder Load

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

  1. Forearm Myofascial Stretches (3 Position: Fingers Back, at a 45 Degree Angle, and Turned in As Much As Possible)
  2. Shoulder ELDOA (2 Position: Fingers Up and Back)
  3. L5/S1 ELDOA
