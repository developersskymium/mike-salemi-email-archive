# Back to balance (essential)

Hi {first_name},

 

What good is a 700 horsepower engine in a Model-T? The suspension, brakes, and probably everything else wouldn’t be able to handle it. 

 

Gyms are full of people training to make a 700 hp engine without realizing the rest of their system can’t express that much energy. 

 

Fundamentals will turn that Model-T into a racecar, but what are the fundamentals??

 

Ask a gym bro and you’ll hear about deadlifts and squats…those are great, but true fundamentals will make you better at both of those lifts plus just about everything else. 

 

This month’s workshop will provide powerful benefits:

Improved stability creating more force generation (be stronger than you look)
Multiply your options for movement and exercise  
A more advanced nervous system that gives you reserves when performing fine motor patterns (perform with more precision longer)  
Not to mention that sports injuries happen late in the game once the nervous system has fatigued. That more advanced nervous system mentioned above is both practical and protective. 

Train This Fundamental

One of the biggest bang for your buck fundamentals is balance.

 

There are few people I've come across that know how to train and teach this topic better than my buddy Warren Williams.

 

I've asked Warren to come teach for us this Saturday, at 8:30amPST and it's going to be EPIC!

 

In this 90 minute virtual workshop you will learn how to maximize a variety of balance tools, progress versions of balance drills in all 3 planes of movement, and functionally incorporate balance training into your fitness regime.

 

This workshop will cover balance training with no equipment, as well as using a series of tools: totally barefoot, swiss ball, bongo board, wobble board, dura discs, hard foam rollers (any of the equipment above is recommended for you to get the most out of this workshop)
