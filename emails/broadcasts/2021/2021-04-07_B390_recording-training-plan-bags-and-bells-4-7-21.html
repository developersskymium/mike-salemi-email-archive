Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (4/7): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (4/8): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

 

1. Biceps Myofascial Stretch

2. Psoas Myofascial Stretch

3. T11/T12 ELDOA

4. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

FRIDAY (4/9): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform 1-3 circuits of the following exercises for 8-10 reps each side, resting as needed in between. Focus on one technique point in each exercise. 
  1. Kettlebell Single Arm Swings OR Floating High Pulls
  2. Bulgarian Bag Back Step (Curtsy Style) Lunges - Bag resting on shoulders

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Psoas Myofascial Stretch

2. T11/T12 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

SATURDAY (4/10): Full Workout

  • Perform the workout exactly as we did on Wednesday. 
  • Aim to increase how well you performed your 5 minute Snatch/floating high pull/swing/deadlift set via progressing the movement, increasing reps per arm, reducing the times the bell rests on the floor, or the amount of hand switches
  • For your Bulgarian Bag circuit, aim to add in one additional round if you'd like and can handle the volume well

 

SUNDAY (4/11): Rest and Play!

  • A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
  • Depending on your goals, as well as how you are feeling, I would suggest the following options to also consider:
    • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes 
    • Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
    • Rowing Machine 500 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time
    • Sprints of 20 yards x 3-8 sets, resting as needed (if you do this, perform the core temperature warm up and joint mobility beforehand)

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Biceps Myofascial Stretch

2. Psoas Myofascial Stretch

3. T11/T12 ELDOA

4. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

MONDAY (4/12): Skill Development #2 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform this sequence below as a continuous circuit (3-6 sets, resting up to 30-45 sec after each circuit).
    1. ​Bulgarian Bag Alt. Curtsy Lunge OR Alt. Overhead Press & Curtsy Lunge x 8-10 each side
    2. Shoulder Front Raises + Hold x (5 reps + 5 sec hold each side)
    3. Inside Grip Bent Over Rows x 10-15

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Biceps Myofascial Stretch

2. Psoas Myofascial Stretch

3. T11/T12 ELDOA

4. L5/S1 ELDOA
