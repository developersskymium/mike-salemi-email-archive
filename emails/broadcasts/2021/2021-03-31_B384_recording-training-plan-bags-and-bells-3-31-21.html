FRIDAY (4/2): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform 1-2 Sets of the following exercises for 8-12 reps, resting as needed in between sets
  1. Spinal Good-mornings x 20-30 reps
  2. Abdominal Roll Ups x 8-15 reps
  3. Side of Hip Raises (any position you'd like to work on) x 10-20 reps
  4. Bulgarian Bag Open Arm Side to Side Swing with Rotation x 10-12 each side
  5. Bulgarian Bag Half Spin OR Alternating Half Swing with Rotational Pivot x 8-10 each side

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Outside Hamstring (Bicep Femoris) Stretch

2. T8/T9 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

SATURDAY (4/3): Full Workout

Perform the workout exactly as we did on Wednesday.

 

 

SUNDAY (3/28): Rest and Play!

  • A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
  • Depending on your goals, as well as how you are feeling, I would suggest the following options to also consider:
    • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes 
    • Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
    • Rowing Machine 500 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time
    • Sprints of 20 yards x 3-8 sets, resting as needed (if you do this, perform the core temperature warm up and joint mobility beforehand)

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Outside Hamstring (Bicep Femoris) Stretch

2. T8/T9 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

MONDAY (3/29): Skill Development #2 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform this sequence below as a continuous circuit (2-4 sets, resting up to 1 minute after each circuit).
    1. ​Bulgarian Bag Half Spin OR Alternating Half Swing with Rotational Pivot x 8-10 each side
    2. Kettlebell Overhead Press OR Static Counter Overhead Press OR Alt. Countered Sea Saw Press x 8-10 each side
    3. Side Of Hip Raises (stick with your weakest position x 15-25 each side
    4. Bent Over Bulgarian Bag Rows x 8-12
    5. Abdominal Roll Ups x 8-15

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Outside Hamstring (Bicep Femoris) Stretch

2. T8/T9 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 ELDOA
