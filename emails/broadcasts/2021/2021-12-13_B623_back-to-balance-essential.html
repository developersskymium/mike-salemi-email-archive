What good is a 700 horsepower engine in a Model-T? The suspension, brakes, and probably everything else wouldn’t be able to handle it.

Fundamentals will turn that Model-T into a racecar, but what are the fundamentals??

 

Ask a gym bro and you’ll hear about deadlifts and squats…those are great, but true fundamentals will make you better at both of those lifts plus just about everything else. 

 

This month’s workshop will provide powerful benefits:

  • Improved stability creating more force generation (be stronger than you look)
  • Multiply your options for movement and exercise  
  • A more advanced nervous system that gives you reserves when performing fine motor patterns (perform with more precision longer)  
  • Not to mention that sports injuries happen late in the game once the nervous system has fatigued. That more advanced nervous system mentioned above is both practical and protective.
