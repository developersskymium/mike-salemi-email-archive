Here is Your Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (12/29): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (12/30): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

  • T4/T5 ELDOA x 1 min each
  • C2/C3 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

FRIDAY (12/31): Skill Development #1

  • Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform the following as one individual station (rest as needed in between):
    • Bulgarian Bag Snatches x 8-10 x 1-2
    • Bulgarian Bag Arm Throws x 8-10 each side x 1-2
    • Bulgarian Bag Lamb Swing or Half Lamb Swing x 8-10 each x 1-3
    • Bodyweight Butterflies x 20-40 x 1-2

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  • T4/T5 ELDOA x 1 min each

  • C2/C3 ELDOA x 1 min

  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

SATURDAY (1/1): Full Workout

 

 

SUNDAY (1/2): Recovery Day

  • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout

Finish with this cool down:

  • T4/T5 ELDOA x 1 min each

  • C2/C3 ELDOA x 1 min

  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

MONDAY (1/3): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

  • Perform the Following as a Circuit for 3-6 rounds, resting up to 60 seconds
  • Bulgarian Bag Lamb Swing or Half Lamb Swing x 8-12 each
  • Bulgarian Bag Thrusters x 10-15
  • Bodyweight Butterflies x 20-30 (active rest and activation exercise)
  • Bulgarian Bag Snatches x 10-15
  • Bulgarian Bag Arm Throws x 8-12 each side
  • Abdominal Roll Ups x 8-15 

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  • T4/T5 ELDOA x 1 min each
  • C2/C3 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

TUESDAY (1/4): Recovery Focus
Perform the cool down from the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

  • T4/T5 ELDOA x 1 min each
  • C2/C3 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min
