# Just for Men - Hold Your Ground Retreat

Hi {first_name},

 

By and large, a man's worth is judged by how well he holds up or contributes to other people’s values... 

 

Working for what the boss wants
Working to get all the stuff a successful man should have
Looking manly 
Being self-reliant 
Being decisive 

Then there are the opposite demands...

 

Caring about time with your family
Cherishing inner values and not worrying about the small stuff
Valuing your mind instead of your body
Being willing to ask for and receive help
Listen well to others and take their desires into account.

None of the things listed above are bad, HOWEVER being a confident man does not and cannot come from a checklist. 

 

Here’s the truth:

 

Until they’re willing to be laughed at, shunned, or even killed for what they value, men won’t live fully.

 

Trusting yourself about what you truly value and not caving to outside pressure is huge for self-respect.

 

How will your life change when you know yourself well enough to say “no” regardless of the pressure?

 

The good news is, you don’t have to wait to develop yourself until you’re in life and death circumstances. What you need is some intentional discomfort in the right places and the support to make new choices when that discomfort arises. 

 

Here is the plan:

Voluntarily face uncomfortable situations
Connect to your deeper strength that can withstand discomfort
Enjoy the self-respect that comes from sticking to what you authentically value

-Mike
