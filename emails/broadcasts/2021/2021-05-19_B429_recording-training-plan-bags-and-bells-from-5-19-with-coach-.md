Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (5/19): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (5/20): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

Joint Mobility for Ankles, Knees & Hips, Shoulders
Frankenstein Leg Kicks
Runners Lunge and Twist
Shin Boxes

 

FRIDAY (5/21): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Perform a 5 mins AMRAP of the following:
Push up to single arm pull:  8-10 Reps
Spin: 10 Reps each side
Goblet squat to bicep curl: 8-10 Reps
If you have the energy, you may perform an additional 5 mins AMRAP of the following:
Low lunge to jump: 5 reps each side
Arm Throw:  5 Reps each side
Suples snatch: 10 Reps

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

Joint Mobility for Ankles, Knees & Hips, Shoulders
Frankenstein Leg Kicks
Runners Lunge and Twist
Shin Boxes

 

SATURDAY (5/22): Full Workout

Perform the workout as on Wednesday. 

 

 

SUNDAY (5/23): Rest and Play! 

A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
Explore the below options depending on your goals and try to remain nasal-diaphragmatic (belly) breathing
Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes 
Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

Joint Mobility for Ankles, Knees & Hips, Shoulders
Frankenstein Leg Kicks
Runners Lunge and Twist
Shin Boxes

 

 

MONDAY (5/24): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

 

Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Perform 1-2 sets of 10 reps of the following exercises as a technical warm up: 
Arm Throw OR Shoulder Load
Half Spin OR Spin with Pause
Goblet Squat
Spins: 100 reps aiming to not drop the Bag (if you were able to do this without setting the Bag down in the prior workout, then once you complete, take 5 minutes rest and you have the option to perform a second set of 50 Spins without setting the Bag down as quickly as possible)

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

Joint Mobility for Ankles, Knees & Hips, Shoulders

Runners Lunge and Twist
Shin Boxes
