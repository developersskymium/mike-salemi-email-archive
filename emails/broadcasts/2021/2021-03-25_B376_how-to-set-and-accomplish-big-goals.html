When I was powerlifting, a big goal meant that I was checked out from every non-essential task for weeks. 

 

I didn’t realize it at the time, but I was teaching myself that big goals are terrible things to go after. I taught myself to hold back because the process of accomplishing an important goal was so uncomfortable. 

 

Yes, I got some big lifts, but what could I have accomplished if I had found how to enjoy the process along the way? 

 

More important than my own goal chasing as a twenty-something year old athlete, what could you accomplish if you enjoyed each small step along the way?

 

The workshop this Saturday will show you how to keep your life and relationships prioritized as you chase down goals. 

 

  1. Stop letting your goals drive you crazy 
  2. Make the process of achievement worthwhile regardless of the outcome (teach yourself to enjoy going after what you want)
  3. CRUSH! 

Imagine arriving at your next goal and letting yourself love the achievement because you’ve enjoyed the process from the start.

 

Some of what you’ll learn:

  • Why goals without values do not work
  • How some of the most common words destroy your ability to stick with goals
  • Why rituals aren’t only for hippies...
