Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (5/12): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (5/13): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

1. Bicep Myofascial Stretch

2. L4/L5 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

FRIDAY (5/14): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform 1-3 Rounds of this Kettlebell Superset, resting up to 1 minute after each round:
  1. Figure 8 to Stand OR Figure 8 To Upper Cut OR Figure 8 to Reverse Lunge Combo x 5-8 reps each side
  2. Overhead Press to Push Press: 3-5 reps + 3-5 reps
  3. Spins OR Half Spins: 8 reps each side

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Bicep Myofascial Stretch

2. L4/L5 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

SATURDAY (5/15): Full Workout

Perform the workout exactly we did on Wednesday. 

 

 

SUNDAY (5/16): Rest and Play! 

  • A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
  • Explore the below options depending on your goals and try to remain nasal-diaphragmatic (belly) breathing
  • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes 
  • Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
  • Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Bicep Myofascial Stretch

2. L4/L5 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

 

MONDAY (5/17): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

 

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform 1-4 Rounds of this Bulgarian Bag Circuit, resting up to 1 minute after each round (note, this is not an AMRAP):
  1. Spins OR Spin with Pause OR Half Spin x 10 reps each side
  2. Side to Side Push Ups OR Classic Push Ups OR Kneeling Push Ups x 10 reps total (5 each side if doing a left and right side option)
  3. Clean + Squat OR Clean x 10 reps

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Bicep Myofascial Stretch

2. L4/L5 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 ELDOA
