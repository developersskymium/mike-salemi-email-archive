Single Arm Kettlebell Snatch OR Single Arm Kettlebell Swings x 5 reps each
Bulgarian Bag Spins OR Half Spins x 6-8 reps each side

Super Set 2: Perform 1 set of the below

Side Lying Inside of Hip (Groin) Raises x 10-15 reps each side
Butterflies x 20-40 reps

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

Biceps Stretch: 45 seconds x 2 sets (each side) with 10 sec rest
Glute Max Deep Stretch: 45 seconds x 2 sets (each side) with 10 sec rest
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

SATURDAY (8/7): Full Workout

Perform the workout as on Wednesday 

 

SUNDAY (8/8): Rest and Play! 

Cycling Sprints - if you've got the energy and want to push it. Cycle easy for 5-10 minutes to start. Then once warm, Sprint for 5-10 sec > Cycle Easy Recovery for 50 sec and repeat this superset 6-10x. Complete this session with a 5-10 minute easy cycling cool down. 
Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout
Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down:

Biceps Stretch: 45 seconds x 2 sets (each side) with 10 sec rest
Glute Max Deep Stretch: 45 seconds x 2 sets (each side) with 10 sec rest
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set
