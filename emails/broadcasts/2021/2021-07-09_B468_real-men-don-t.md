# Real Men Don’t….

Hi {first_name},

 

This is the start of a line I imagine you’ve heard at one time or another. Maybe it was in a movie, during a TV show, or maybe it was even at home.

 

A few common ways I’ve heard this phrase conclude are:

Real men don’t show weakness
Real men don’t express their emotions
Real men don’t cry
And the list goes on...

Well, what does being “real” man mean anyway?

 

My answer? 

 

I don't have the slightest clue lol.

 

BUT, what I do know to be true in my heart, is that a “balanced” man looks and feels like one who embodies:

 

Strength AND Vulnerability

 

I bring this up here because I’m going to share something that in fact scares a part of me to be more vocal about...

 

As you may already know, for so long much of my identity was wrapped up as being an athlete.

 

When I stopped competing 4 years ago, a part of me felt lost and without direction. 

 

During this time, I did, however, find tremendous purpose in teaching.

 

Additionally, as I grew, I also found great fulfillment in holding space and supporting people in their growth in areas outside of physical training.

 

In this moment, I am personally at a transition point in my life. 

 

While I LOVE teaching all things physical movement, I also have felt a deep calling to energize more things that bring our mind, emotions, and spirit into harmony - so that we can be less crushed by our limiting beliefs and live in a way that expresses greater internal freedom.

 

Stay tuned for more content that will not only enhance the ways you train more intentionally, but enliven your entire being.
