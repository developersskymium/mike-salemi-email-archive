Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (7/28): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (7/29): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

Quad Myofascial Stretch: 30 seconds x 3 sets (each side)
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

FRIDAY (7/30): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Perform the following superset for 2-4 rounds, resting as needed in between sets (up to 60 sec):
Single Side Bulgarian Bag Lamb Swing OR Alternating Lamb Swings x 8-10-10 each side
Side Lying Groin Raises x 10-20 each side 
Butterflies: 15-25

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

Quad Myofascial Stretch: 30 seconds x 3 sets (each side)
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

SATURDAY (7/31): Full Workout

Perform the workout as on Wednesday 

 

SUNDAY (8/1): Rest and Play! 

Cycling Sprints - if you've got the energy and want to push it. Cycle easy for 5-10 minutes to start. Then once warm, Sprint for 5-10 sec > Cycle Easy Recovery for 50 sec and repeat this superset 6-10x. Complete this session with a 5-10 minute easy cycling cool down. 
Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout
Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down:

Quad Myofascial Stretch: 30 seconds x 3 sets (each side)
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

MONDAY (8/2): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

Circuit: Perform 2-5 rounds of the four exercises below back-to-back, resting up to 60 seconds after each circuit:

Kettlebell Static Split Squat Overhead Press x 5-6 each side (going heavier weight than what you did in our last group class. 5-6 reps is going to be more of a strength focus for the upper body. If you don't have a heavier load, perform 6-10 reps each side, and move slowly on the raising and lowering phases)
Side Lying Groin Raises x 10-20 each side 
Butterflies: 15-25

*Once you've completed the above circuits, rest as needed, and then perform 20-30 TOTAL reps each side of Bulgarian Bag Lamb Swings. You can split this up into as many or as few sets as you would like, resting as needed in between. Since this is more of a finisher, aim to finish it as quickly as you can while maintaining nasal breathing.

Finish with this cool down:

Quad Myofascial Stretch: 30 seconds x 3 sets (each side)
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.
