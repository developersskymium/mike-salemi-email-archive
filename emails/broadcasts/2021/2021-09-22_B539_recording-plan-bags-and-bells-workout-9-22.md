Here is Your Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (9/22): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (9/23): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

FRIDAY (9/24): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Perform the below, with the mouth fully closed throughout
Bodyweight Half OR Full Get Ups x 25 each side (broken up however yo'd like), ideally with as little rest as needed. What you can do as an idea, is alternate left and right sides every 1-5 reps depending on your fatigue level.

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

SATURDAY (9/25): Full Workout

Perform the workout as on Wednesday
You can increase the rounds of the last exercise circuit we did (Modified knees over toes lunges, Rows, and Ab work), to be between 2-5 rounds. 

 

SUNDAY (9/26): Active Rest (OR Complete Off/Play Day)

Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout

Finish with this cool down:

T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

MONDAY (9/27): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

Perform the circuit below 2-5x.
After each circuit, rest for 60 seconds in order to recover. You want to enter the Swings feeling fresh, so if you need to rest up to 2 minutes, in order to stay nasal breathing and feel powerful, take that additional rest is my suggestion. 
KB Pivot Swing OR KB Single Arm Swing OR Single Arm Deadlift x 10/10 
KB Half TGU with Presses OR Half TGU OR Bodyweight Half TGU x 1-3 each
KB Bent Over Open Palm Row x 15 slow tempo throughout
BB or KB or Bodweight Modified Knees Over Toes Lunges x 10/10

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

TUESDAY (9/28): Recovery Focus
Perform the cool down from the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

T8/T9 ELDOA x 1 min
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min
