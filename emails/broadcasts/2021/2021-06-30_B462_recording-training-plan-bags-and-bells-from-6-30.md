Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (6/30): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (7/1): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

L3/L4 ELDOA x 1 min
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

FRIDAY (7/1): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
Perform the following (you may complete the below in any format that you'd like - circuit (back to back exercises for a specified number of reps), station training (each individual exercise completed by itself before moving to the next), or a mix of the two as you see fit. Remain nasal breathing throughout.
Bodyweight Turkish Get Ups (TGU). Perform a total of 10-15 reps each side (level I), OR 20-30 (level II) reps each side 
Two Hand Kettlebell Swings (focus on technique and power production) or Two Hand Deadlfits for Level I: 5-15 reps x 2 sets
Bulgarian Bag Side to Side Swing Squats x 6-10 each side x 1-2 sets

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

L3/L4 ELDOA x 1 min
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

SATURDAY (7/3): Full Workout

Perform the workout as on Wednesday 

 

SUNDAY (7/4): Rest and Play! 

Cycling Sprints if you've got juice in the tank and want to push it. Cycle easy for 5-10 minutes to start. Then once warm, Sprint for 5-10 sec > Cycle Easy Recovery for 50 sec and repeat this superset 6-10x. Complete this session with a 5-10 minute easy cycling cool down. 
Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout
Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down:

L3/L4 ELDOA x 1 min
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

MONDAY (7/5): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

Perform the following circuits as laid out below:
Superset (aim for 3-8 rounds, resting as needed to maintain nasal breathing)
Bodyweight OR Single KB OR Double KB TGU - Combat Stance Version that we did in class x 1 rep each side (if you are performing the bodyweight version, perform 2 reps each side)
Deadlift OR Two Hand Swing OR Single Arm Swings OR Double Bell Swings x 8-15 reps (for single arm swing, aim to perform 8 reps each arm)

Finish with this cool down:

L3/L4 ELDOA x 1 min
L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min
