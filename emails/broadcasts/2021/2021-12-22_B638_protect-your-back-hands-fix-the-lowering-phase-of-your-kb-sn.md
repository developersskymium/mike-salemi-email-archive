# Protect Your Back & Hands: FIX the Lowering Phase of Your KB Snatch

Hi {first_name},

 

Tons can go wrong as you lower the kettlebell when performing a snatch. As you become more technically proficient in the lowering phase, you’ll get far more benefit and experience what this lift was actually designed to deliver. Instead of recovering from torn hands and a sore lower back, you’ll be accumulating quality volume, a bigger gas tank, more powerful "go" muscles (muscles on the backside of the body), and grip.

 

In this week’s video, I teach 2 styles to lower the kettlebell.

 

1

Over the top - the simplest method

 

2

Corkscrew - more technical, but more efficient 

 

Both methods are great IF you get it right.

 

Whichever method you use, practice at a lighter weight to lay down quality reps. It can take some practice to get the feel for these movements. Keep at it and soon it will be second nature.
