Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (4/28): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (4/29): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

1. Oblique Stretch

2. T8/T9 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 EDLOA

 

 

FRIDAY (4/30): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform 2-4 supersets of the following exercises. These are done every minute on the minute (EMOM), thus when you finish your reps, the remaining seconds left until the next minute begins is your rest.

  • Kettlebell Deadlift OR Two Hand Swing OR Two Hand Multi-Directional Walking Swing 

    • (x 10-12 Reps Level I    /   x 20-25 Reps Level II)

  • Kettlebell Half Kneeling Single Arm Overhead Press OR Single Arm Straddle Press 

    • (x 6-8 Reps Each Arm Level I    /   x 8-12 Reps Each Arm Level II)

 

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Oblique Stretch

2. T8/T9 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 EDLOA

 

 

SATURDAY (5/1): Full Workout

  • Perform the workout exactly as Bridgette led you through on Wednesday. 

 

SUNDAY (5/2): Rest and Play! 

  • A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
  • Explore the below options depending on your goals and try to remain nasal-diaphragmatic (belly) breathing
    • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes 
    • Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
    • Rowing Machine 500 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Oblique Stretch

2. T8/T9 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 EDLOA

 

 

MONDAY (5/3): Skill Development #2 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform 2-4 Rounds of the following exercises. These are done every minute on the minute (EMOM), thus when you finish your reps, the remaining seconds left until the next minute begins is your rest.

  • Bulgarian Bag Open Arm Side to Side Swing OR Half Spin OR Spin-Half Spin Combo 

    • (x 6-8 Reps Each Side Level I    /   x 5-6 Reps Each Side Level II)

  • Bulgarian Bag Alternating Curtsy Squat: SLOW Going Down, HOLD at Bottom, FAST Up

    • (x 8-10 Reps Level I    /   x 8-12 Reps Level II)

  • Bulgarian Bag Drop and Catch, "2 on 1"

    • (x 20-24 Reps Level I    /   x 34-38 Reps Level II)

  • Bulgarian Bag Bent Over Row: SLOW Going Down, SLOW Up
    • (x 5-8 Reps Level I    /   x 6-10 Reps Level II)

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

1. Oblique Stretch

2. T8/T9 ELDOA

3. L5/S1 EDLOA
