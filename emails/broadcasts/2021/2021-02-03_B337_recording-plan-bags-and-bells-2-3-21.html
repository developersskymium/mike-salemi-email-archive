Weekly Training Plan Recommendations:

 

WEDNESDAY (2/3): Full Workout

**Note — Towel Drill: remembering which arm the towel should be located gave people trouble today. Please to always place the towel in the armpit of the loaded arm, NOT the unloaded for this to be effective. (Reference: 24:41) 

 

THURSDAY (2/4): Stretching Focus

Perform the stretches from yesterday demonstrated at the end of our session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to these stretches.

 

1. *Gracilis Myofascial Stretch

2. L5/S1 ELDOA

 

*Add one additional set to the Gracilis stretch, and perform 3 sets of 30 seconds with a 10 second rest in between on each side.

 

FRIDAY (2/5): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

 

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Focus on Exercise #1 & #2 in Circuit #1 from our training this week (Level I, this will be either the Waiters Clean with Towel to Forward Lunge (or any regression shown), and for Level II, this will be a Snatch into Forward Lunge (or any regression shown).
  • With this main movement, you have two options to work with - either increase the repetitions by 2-4, thus between 10-14 on each side, (this would be more of a conditioning focus). You may be able to stay at the same weight, or reduce it by one kettlebell weight if you have it.  OR you can choose to do the same reps that we performed. OR choose to reduce the reps by 2-4, thus between 4-6 on each side (this would be more of a strength focus) and increase the load.
  • After you perform this exercise, superset it with Butterflies for 15-25 reps
  • Perform this superset for 2-4 sets. However, be sure to save some energy for tomorrow full workout.
  • Finish with the L5/S1 ELDOA for 1 minute and rest well before your full workout tomorrow. 

 

 

SATURDAY (2/6): Full Workout

Perform the workout exactly as we did on Wednesday. 

 

If you are able to progress, explore so in the following ways:

  1. Add 1-4 repetitions to each main exercise
  2. Explore progressing to the next skill progression (based on where you finished on Wednesday)
  3. Add 1-2 sets to one or both circuits
  4. Add weight (if this is an option for you based on what you have access to at home)

 

 

SUNDAY (2/7): Rest and Play!

A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 

 

Depending on your goals, as well as how you are feeling, I would suggest the following options to also consider:

 

  • Hiking for ~45 minutes (long duration cardio, moderate walking pace)
  • Rowing Machine ~8-15 minutes (cardio focus, moderate pace)
  • Meditation 5-20 minutes (especially if you are feeling physically or mentally stressed)
