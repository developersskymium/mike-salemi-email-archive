Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (7/7): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (7/8): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

  1. Lat Myofascial Strech x 30 sec x 3 sets
  2. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

FRIDAY (7/9): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform the following superset for 2-3 rounds, resting as needed in between sets:
  1. Single Arm Kettlebell Overhead Press OR RMT Step Press x 6-10 reps (light Weight)
  2. Bent Over Kettlebell Open Palm Grip Row x 8-10 reps with a 10 second Hold

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  1. Lat Myofascial Strech x 30 sec x 3 sets
  2. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

SATURDAY (7/10): Full Workout

  • Perform the workout as on Wednesday 

 

SUNDAY (7/11): Rest and Play! 

  • Cycling Sprints if you've got juice in the tank and want to push it. Cycle easy for 5-10 minutes to start. Then once warm, Sprint for 5-10 sec > Cycle Easy Recovery for 50 sec and repeat this superset 6-10x. Complete this session with a 5-10 minute easy cycling cool down. 
  • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout
  • Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
  • Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down:

  1. Lat Myofascial Strech x 30 sec x 3 sets
  2. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

 

MONDAY (7/12): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

  • Perform the following circuits as laid out below:
  • Superset the following for 2-4 rounds, resting as needed to feel fully recovered before coming back to the Hammer Snatches
  1. ​Bulgarian Bag Hammer Snatch x 5
  2. Bulgarian Bag Lamb Swing x 8-10 each side
  • Once you've completed the above perform, the following superset for 2-4 rounds, resting as needed in between:
  • 1. Bent Over Kettlebell Open Palm Grip Row x 8-20 reps with a 10-15 second Hold
  • 2. Wall Sits, Max Duration Hold (remember to lower down to come out of the position once you've hit your fatigue threshold) 

Finish with this cool down:

  1. Lat Myofascial Strech x 30 sec x 3 sets
  2. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min x 1 set

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.
