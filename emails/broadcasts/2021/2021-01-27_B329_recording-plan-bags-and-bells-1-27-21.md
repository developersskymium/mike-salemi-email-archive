SATURDAY (1/30): Full Workout

Perform the workout exactly as we did on Wednesday.

Add 1-4 repetitions to each exercise
Explore progressing to the next skill progression (based on where you finished on Wednesday)
Add 1-2 sets to one or both circuits

SUNDAY (1/31): Rest and Play!

If you do decide to perform cardio, I highly suggest doing your Core Temperature Warm Up and Bodyweight Mobility series from the workout prior to cardio.
