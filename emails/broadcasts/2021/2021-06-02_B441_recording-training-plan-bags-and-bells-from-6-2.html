Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (6/2): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (6/3): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down from demonstrated at the end of our live training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down.

  1. Feet Up the Wall:  for this one (we did not demo this specifically), simply lay for 2-5+ minutes with your back flat on the floor and your feet resting up the wall. You'll want to straighten your legs just to the point that you feel a gentle stretch behind the thighs. From here simply breathe deeply. This is a regression to the L5/S1 ELDOA and a part of the starting place for it. This gentle stretch is highly beneficial to go for longer durations at very light effort.
  2. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 minute

 

FRIDAY (6/4): Skill Development #1 and Stretching

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform Any Of the Following Exercise Progressions. Since you have a full workout, tomorrow, complete 1-2 sets of the below for 3-10 reps depending on the exercise and get ready to crush the full workout tomorrow.  
  1. Two Hand Deadlift
  2. Two Hand Swing 
  3. Single Arm Swing
  4. Single Arm Staggered Stance Swing
  5. Chamberlin Squat OR Chamberlin Squat and Push Press
  6. Half Windmill OR Half Windmill to Elbow

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

  1. Feet Up the Wall:  for this one (we did not demo this specifically), simply lay for 2-5+ minutes with your back flat on the floor and your feet resting up the wall. SATURDAY (6/5): Full Workout

  • Perform the workout as on Wednesday and aim to get more volume in during your Kettlebell AMRAP, Spin Ladder, and Abdominal Roll Up.

 

 

SUNDAY (6/6): Rest and Play! 

  • A great option today would be to get outside for unstructured, unbound play. 
  • Explore the below options depending on your goals and try to remain nasal-diaphragmatic (belly) breathing
  • Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes 
  • Swimming: 15-20 minutes of pure unstructured movement in the water. Aim to move, wiggle all your joints, and play in the water freely. This is one of the best forms of recovery I've experienced personally over the years. 
  • Rowing Machine 500-1000 meter row, while staying nasal breathing the entire time

Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:

  1. L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 minite

 

 

MONDAY (6/7): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

 

  • Perform both the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform a 5 minute Kettlebell AMRAP: Swings + Chamberlin Squats + Half Windmill
  • Perform 1-2 Sets of the Bulgarian Bag Spin Ladder (rest as needed in between
  • Perform 1-2 Max Technique Sets on the Abdominal Roll Ups
  • Finish with this cool down as performed in the workout:
