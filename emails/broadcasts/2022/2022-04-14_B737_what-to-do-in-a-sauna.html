B737 · What to do in a sauna

Hi {first_name},

 

Saunas are a fantastic training and recovery tool. 

 

Yes, you can do some gentle training in a sauna (watch the video for precautions!), but simply allowing your body to adjust to being in such a hot environment has a training effect. 

 

The human body adapts to its environment. Modern life has us primarily in comfortable, climate-controlled situations, which means the body doesn’t have to be as robust as it can be. Simply sitting in a sauna allows your body to adapt. You literally add capacity to your body by sitting in a hot room. It’s amazing. 

 

If you have access to a sauna or are interested in it, this video is for you!

 

Less is more in a sauna so be sure to proceed with caution. Watch this week’s video for my thoughts on how to make great use of a sauna. 

 

*The sauna I use is from Divine Saunas.
