# What makes a “killer death” grip?

Hello {first_name}, 

 

If you’re like me, the idea of doing isolated grip training sounds too boring for me to program it into my training. The Bulgarian Bag is the perfect way to train grip while doing dynamic, full body movements. 

 

Here’s the main reason why:

 

Those handles… 

Your hands will get sweaty. At the same time, you’re spinning the bag 360 degrees which creates a lot of force pulling the bag away from you. You have to squeeze hard and keep focus on maintaining that killer death grip or the bag will go flying.
