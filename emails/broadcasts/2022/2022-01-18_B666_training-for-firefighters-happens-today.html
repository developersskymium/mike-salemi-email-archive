Quick note for firefighters.

 

The free training today (register and you’ll get the recording if you can’t make it live) will give you a strategy for how to improve your breathing. Laying on the floor sucking in air after a hard workout is NOT breath training! 

 

You’ll learn how to get more oxygen in your blood and then to your tissues (where it counts) while using a lower volume of air. Simply put, your oxygen tank will last longer, you’ll be able to work better, and you’ll have clearer thinking. 

 

The training alone is enough for you to make big improvements. And for those interested in more, a fitness program centering around breathwork, unconventional tools, and recovery will be offered at the end - zero obligation.
