Here is Your Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (1/19): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (1/20): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

  • L2/L3 ELDOA x 1 min

  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

FRIDAY (1/21): Skill Development #1

  • Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform the following as individual stations:
    • Bulgarian Bag Alternating Spins x 1L/1R for 4-6 each side x 1-3 sets
      • For this, you will perform one rep of a spin to the left, then immediately transition to the right for one spin. You will do this until you've completed 4-6 each side. The idea here is to improve how quickly and efficiently you can switch sides so that tomorrow when you perform your spin test you will reduce your time here. 
    • Butterflies x 20-50 x 1-3
    • Side of Hip Raises (Glute Medius) x 20-40 each x 1-3

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  • L2/L3 ELDOA x 1 min

  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

SATURDAY (1/22): Full Workout

 

 

SUNDAY (1/23): Recovery Day

Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout

Finish with this cool down:

  • L2/L3 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

MONDAY (1/24): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

  • Perform the Following:
  • Bulgarian Bag Spin Test Full x 10L/10R + 10 Push Ups x 5 Rounds

Once Completed with the Spin Test, Perform This Circuit:

  1. Kettlebell Figure 8 Progression (work up into 2-5 working sets total at whatever progression you feel is right for you today), resting as needed:
    1. Figure 8 x 6-8 each 
    2. Figure 8 to Stand x 6-8 each
    3. Fiture 8 to Upper Cut x 6-8 each
    4. Figure 8 to Squat (Level II) 6-8 each
    5. Figure 8 to Back Step Lunge (Level II) x 6-8 each
  2. Abdominal Roll Ups with Rotation (Position Torsion x 10-20 total (if you are doing these with rotation, do half the reps to one side, and the other half to the other)
  3. Kettlebell Bent Over Rows with Reciprocity x 8-12 each

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  • I would suggest doing a stretch for chest and biceps today (while we did not get to that in class). 3 sets of 30 seconds (each side for chest) with 10 second rest for these.

  • L2/L3 ELDOA x 1 min

  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

TUESDAY (1/25): Recovery Focus
Perform the cool down from the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

  • L2/L3 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

Enjoy and crush those spin tests!
