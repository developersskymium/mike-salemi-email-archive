# Some of the most courageous men are cowards

Hi {first_name},

 

Heads Up: this email is specifically for the men on my list

 

The respected American poet, Robert Bly had powerful medicine for men. 

 

Watch this ~30 second clip

 

It's no shock that the feelings men often face around inadequacy are not pleasant ones. As a result, numbness can often take place inside. 

 

For so many of us men, this rings true. 

 

Inadequacy is enough to shut a man down. On the other hand, inadequacy is powerful enough to drive a man to high levels of achievement…but what we don't often see is that it comes at a cost.

 

The solution to the issue CANNOT be:

Do more
Follow this formula
Work harder
Suck it up
Copy the success of someone else
Etc. 

The solution is connecting to ourselves on an increasingly truthful level. 

 

There are principles for connecting to oneself, but the key is that everyone has to do it in their own way - authentic to them.

 

If a formula does help, it does so because the formula serves you. 

 

Remember this, you supersede any formula that someone may tell you.

 

We fellas tend to confuse this. We push for formulas and things like them because it allows us to operate and carry on while still being numb. 

 

In my experience, the way to address the sense of inadequacy is to learn to feel a wider spectrum of feelings, and to accept them as a natural part of being human.

 

The feelings that hurt, that ache, that are scary, are just there, AND the fun, exciting, and joyful feelings are there too. They come together as a package deal (emotions = energy in motion).

 

When you are no longer afraid of your feelings, you are in greater touch with yourself.

 

You are in touch with yourself because you are listening.

 

Of course, it doesn't happen all at once. It’s a process that continues with time. 

 

As the Men of Movement retreat comes up in May, we aim to create a container that allows men an opportunity to get reps at connecting with themselves. The activities, as well as the other men present moving towards a common goal, are there all help open to what's really going on inside.

 

We practice what is possible. 

 

Just like squatting with a barbell can feel awkward and uncomfortable when you first start. Practicing intentional reps and good technique, we are better able to make most any exercise feel great. 

 

The goal of the Men of Movement retreat is to create a space for men to rewire and re-pattern their sovereignty through body, breath, and brotherhood. 

 

Hold your ground.

Know who you are and what you want.

Together we move.

Together we rise.
