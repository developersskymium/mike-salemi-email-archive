Here’s what I believe about breath training for firefighters:

 

Every firefighter should know how to improve their lung capacity because your life could depend on it.

 

Being strong is important. Knowing the technical specifics of your job is vital. Breathing makes it possible.

 

Everything - especially under stress - is improved by training your breath.

  • Improved lung capacity
  • Reduced requirement of oxygen for the same amount of work
  • Clearer thinking because your body is less in a stressed state
  • And a whole ton more

Controlling your breath is how to keep your mind calm to focus on your task.

  • How to use breath holds to increase CO2 tolerance and get more oxygen into the blood and to your tissues
  • The benefits of nasal breathing and exercises to use
  • Strategy for breath recovery
