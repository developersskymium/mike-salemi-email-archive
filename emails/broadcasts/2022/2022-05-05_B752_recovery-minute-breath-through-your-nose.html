Hello {first_name},

 

Often, it's the simple things that have the biggest impact. Breathing through your nose (NOT your mouth) may seem small but the benefits are huge.

 

Why does this small change have such a big impact?

 

  • Because it affects how oxygen is delivered to your tissues
  • Because it has a direct impact on your nervous system
  • Because you breathe thousands of times every day

Closing your mouth is a small change that carries BIG benefits. 

 

Breathing through your nose helps put your body into a parasympathetic state where it is better suited to recover. The fanciest post-workout protein shake can’t do that, but you can simply by closing your mouth. 

 

To start, focus on breathing through your nose in these 3 situations:

 

  1. During the day
  2. During conditioning workouts (especially during warm-ups)
  3. While sleeping

I know…during the day and while sleeping pretty much means all the time! And while there are a few times when it's ok to breathe through your mouth while conditioning, those instances are rare, so basically keep it nasal as much as you can.

 

Here are a few of the benefits:

 

  • Greater ability to enter into a flow state
  • Increased work capacity
  • Improved recovery and restful sleep

Nasal breathing is your recovery minute for this week. Make it a habit.
