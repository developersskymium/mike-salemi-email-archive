**Free training on Jan. 18th specifically for firefighters**

 

Everything - especially under stress - is improved by training your breath.

 

  • Improved lung capacity
  • Reduced use of oxygen for the same amount of work
  • Clearer thinking because your body is less stressed

Firefighting isn’t like a sport with an off season. You can’t plan your training to be at peak performance for the big call. 

 

Instead, you need strategies that improve your foundational readiness without causing wear and tear on your body. 

 

Register for this free training. You’ll have everything you need to begin improving your use of oxygen by the end of the training. 

 

  • Establish your baseline
  • How to use breath holds to increase CO2 tolerance and get more oxygen into the blood
  • The benefits of nasal breathing and exercises to use
  • Strategy for breath recovery 

The training alone is enough for you to make big improvements. And for those interested in more, a fitness program centering around breathwork, unconventional tools, and recovery will be offered at the end - zero obligation.
