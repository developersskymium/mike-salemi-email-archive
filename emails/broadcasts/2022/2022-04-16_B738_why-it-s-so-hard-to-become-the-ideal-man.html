B738 · Why it’s so hard to become the “ideal” man

Hi {first_name},

 

Heads Up: this email is specifically for the men on my list

 

Many cultural factors teach men that they are supposed to be able to solve virtually any problem on their own (or be so financially successful they can pay for the best to come and solve it…or be so good at tons of skills they can figure it out in a frugal way). 

 

What happens as every man knows they aren’t actually able to live up to this image of what a man “should” be?

 

They come to feel inadequate.

 

Then they hide that feeling of inadequacy…

 

Through high performance

Through low performance

And everything in between

 

Really, we men find anything to avoid those uncomfortable feelings. But learning to feel is what allows a man to connect to himself.

 

We cannot feel what it is to be ourselves until we allow ourselves to feel.

 

Anything else is creating a mental model of what you think “yourself” should be and trying to live that model out. 

 

Inadequacy often comes from not being able to make yourself be who you aren’t.

You will never be who you aren’t. 

 

The Plan:

Connect to yourself.

Learn to feel.

Feel the grief & feel the pain.

 

Then you’ll learn through experience -step by step- to feel the strength of who YOU are rather than inadequacy for who you aren’t.
