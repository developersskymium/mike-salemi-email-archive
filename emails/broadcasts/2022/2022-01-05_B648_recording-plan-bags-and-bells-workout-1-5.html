Here is Your Weekly Recommended Training Plan:

 

WEDNESDAY (1/5): Full Workout

 

 

THURSDAY (1/6): Recovery Focus

Perform the cool down the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

  • Forearm Flexor Stretch x 45 sec
  • Forearm Extensor Stretch x 45 sec
  • Quad Stretch x 1 min each
  • T7/T8 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

FRIDAY (1/7): Skill Development #1

  • Perform the Core Temperature Warm Up and/or the Joint Warm Up to start
  • Perform the following as one individual station or as a circuit, resting as needed, and focusing on technique and feeling an activation in the chest and back with the push ups and rows mentioned below. 
    • Bulgarian Bag Alternating Half Spin to Pivot Lunge x 6-10 each x 1-2
    • Push Up x 6-8 x 1-2
    • Bent Over Row x 8-10 x 1-2

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  • Forearm Flexor Stretch x 45 sec
  • Forearm Extensor Stretch x 45 sec
  • Quad Stretch x 1 min each
  • T7/T8 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

SATURDAY (1/8): Full Workout

 

 

SUNDAY (1/9): Recovery Day

Easy walk outside/in nature (or anytime of day too) around your neighborhood for ~20-60 minutes remaining in nasal breathing throughout

Finish with this cool down:

  • Quad Stretch x 1 min each

  • T7/T8 ELDOA x 1 min

  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

MONDAY (1/10): Skill Development #2, Conditioning, and Stretching

  • Perform the Following Supersets for 2-4 rounds each, resting up to 60 seconds

SUPERSET #1

  1. Bulgarian Bag Half Spins OR Spin-Side Swing SQ Combo x 8-12 each
  2. Bulgarian Bag Push Ups x 8-15

SUPERSET #2

  1. Bulgarian Bag Swing Squats x 10-15
  2. Bulgarian Bag Military Presses x 10-15

SUPERSET #3

  1. Bulgarian Bag Alt. Shoulder Load x 10-15 each side
  2. Bulgarian Bag Bent Over Rows x 10-15 

Finish with this cool down as done in the workout:

  • Forearm Flexor Stretch x 45 sec

  • Forearm Extensor Stretch x 45 sec

  • Quad Stretch x 1 min each

  • T7/T8 ELDOA x 1 min

  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min

 

TUESDAY (1/11): Recovery Focus
Perform the cool down from the videoed training session. Depending on your time availability, you can either solely do the core temperature warm up (marching in place), or if you have more time, also perform the Joint Warm Up as well prior to this cool down:

  • Forearm Flexor Stretch x 45 sec
  • Forearm Extensor Stretch x 45 sec
  • Quad Stretch x 1 min each
  • T7/T8 ELDOA x 1 min
  • L5/S1 ELDOA x 1 min
