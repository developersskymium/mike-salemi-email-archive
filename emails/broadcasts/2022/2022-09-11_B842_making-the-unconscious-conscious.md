# Making The Unconscious Conscious

Hi {first_name},

 

As Carl Jung put it, “until you make the unconscious conscious, it will direct your life and you will call it fate."

 

(mic drop)

 

As a coach, so much of what we do is to support clients through this very process.

 

But this doesn’t only apply to client-coach relationships - bringing the unconscious conscious, which essentially heightens our level of self-awareness, applies to all people and relationships.

 

With this topic in mind, tomorrow at 5pm PST, I’ll be bringing on coach and friend, Jason Olivea for an IG Live interview. Jason co-developed a powerful personality assessment tool called InnerCompass. This is something I first took with Jason over 2 years ago, and the clarity and empowerment that both Lauren and I have received from it has been very helpful in our relationship, as well as our personal and professional lives.

 

The InnerCompass Personality Assessment invites people to come into contact with their core drives and motivations, which largely lie in the unconscious, per Carl Jung quotes above. We need a way to consider with curiosity and courage our shadow and habitual patterns which had served us in historical phases of life and yet do not serve us today due to the very different circumstances and challenges we face with life today.

 

The InnerCompass Assessment offers a Roadmap to navigate our inner life (i.e. the way we talk to ourselves and perceive the world) in a way that actually feels like I have a way to move forward without bypassing and divorcing the past.
