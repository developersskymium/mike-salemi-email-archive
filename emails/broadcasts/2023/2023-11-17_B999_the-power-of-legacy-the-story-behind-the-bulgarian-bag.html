The Origin Story

The journey of the Bulgarian Bag began in Marquette, Michigan, 15+ years ago where Ivan Ivanov, while coaching at the Olympic Training Center Campus for Wrestlers, sought a unique training tool that could help his needs as a coach.

 

His vision was ambitious - he needed:

  • To train his athletes in a way, functionally, that no other tool could - suitable for multi-pattern and plane movements used in wrestling
  • To build high levels of muscular endurance
  • A versatile implement that would not damage floors/mats
  • Something that could be used with little to no set-up, both during and after practice
  • A way to foster team camaraderie and competitiveness while conditioning
  • A tool that allowed his creativity to be unleashed and design innovative, fun, and challenging workout protocols

 

The result?

 

A testament to Ivan's dedication, the Bulgarian Bag emerged as not just a product, but a culmination of blood, sweat, and tears. Ivan not only created a revolutionary piece of fitness and performance equipment but also developed time-tested training methodologies.

Suples isn't just a global company, it is a passionate family business. Having experienced the dynamics of a family-run business myself for much of my life, I understand the commitment and sacrifices that go into every endeavor.

The Authenticity Factor

While there may be knock-off bags out there, there is only one Original Bulgarian Bag. Crafted in Bulgaria at their factory run by Ivan's brother, these bags carry with them not just the product, but the essence of a culture steeped in human potential and teamwork.

 

More Than a Product

The Bulgarian Bag is not just a fitness tool; it's an entryway into a rich history of individuals striving for performance, health, and fitness together. Being a part of this story and lineage means something profound.
