# When frustration wins, THIS happens

Hi {first_name},

 

Have you ever said, or felt the following:

 

"I am frustrated, I’m trying so hard, but the people in my life don’t really seem to see me."

 

"No matter what I do, it’s not enough."

 

"The same shitty patterns from past relationships keep happening and I don’t know what to do."

 

Want to know something?

 

In addition to having conversations each day with men, experiencing many of these exact challenges, I too, have experienced every single one of them at some point in my life.

 

And I can say that when any one of these frustrations continues over time, they can turn into anger.

 

Anger at yourself, and anger sometimes at the people closest to you.

 

And what happens next?

 

When this becomes repressed, next comes resentment.

 

And resentment is not a fun place to be.

 

While these emotions are understandable, valid, and a part of the human experience, it’s when we get stuck there, and don’t know how to navigate our way through it that stuff gets really tough. 

 

But it doesn’t have to be this way.

 

You DO have a choice, although it may not seem like it when you're going through it.

 

I'd like to share something with you.

 

Something one of my first kettlebell coaches over 15 years ago told me something that I believe was instrumental in my success as a competitive lifter.

 

And it was this: “trust the process.”

 

In the years that followed, I chose to add a few things:

 

Trust the process AND... 

 

Be curious about everything.

 

Use discernment.

 

Have the courage to take the first step.

 

In this way, sooner or later shifts do happen.

 

That last one, “have the courage to take the first step", is critical, because without it, nothing else happens, and life will remain the same.

 

And I know even when taking the first step, we all need a little help sometimes.

 

Well, that in mind, here is your personal invitation - take this next step with me, and I can say with confidence it will be the best decision of your life.

 

How do I know?

 

Because this is the stuff I live for, and what I do, and see, day in and day out in my practice.

 

So if you’re feeling the call, check out The Path to Inspired Action. A 6-week deep dive, virtual group coaching program I’ve created for men like you.

 

Click here to learn more, apply today, and hop on a call with me directly.

 

Let’s FOOK’N GO!

Mike
