In this episode of The Path Podcast, I've brought on Conner Moore, the creative director and co-founder of Soulfire Productions. Like myself, Conner is a new dad - his honest perspective and ability to call it like he sees it, in my opinion, are two of the things that make him stand out in the world in a noticeable way. In this episode, our main focus is on the transformative journey of fatherhood, exploring how it simultaneously softens and toughens us. Conner also opens up about the physical and emotional challenges he and his wife faced during their IVF journey. We talk about the most essential skills for being a great father, how important it is to integrate our past experiences to become better parents, and raising capable children.
