# Upcoming Men's Retreat, You're Invited

Men of Movement Alumni!

 

I hope each of you are doing awesome!

 

As you may be aware, the next Men of Movement Retreat is coming up June 8-11th back out in Mount Shasta.

 

While you can expect key aspects similar to past events (Sweat Lodge, breathwork, and Ya'acov's Movement Medicine Ceremony for example) there are LOTS that will be new and different at this upcoming one. I believe we have 9 new sessions!

 

Getting in touch with the wisdom held inside the body through unique embodiment work, specialised breathwork practices, physicality exercises, the Enneagram, and much more will all be intertwined at this event. 

 

It's a time with no cell phones, to get out in nature with our brothers, and dive in together so that we can experience greater connection, healing, and growth. 

 

If you are feeling the call to join us, I'd love to have you. I will be offering this at a discounted rate for past participants. If you are interested please respond to this email and I can get you more information. 

 

Wishing you and your families all my best and big love,

Mike
