In recent years, the fitness industry has seen a surge in the term "movement," largely signifying a shift from machine-based, isolated workouts to more functional training approaches.

 

Movement is about integrating diverse physical activities that respect real-life motions, emphasizing core strength, flexibility, balance, and coordination - just to name a few.

 

To be movement-minded in my opinion, means focusing on holistic fitness routines that enhance overall functionality, adaptability, and agility for better everyday challenges.

 

If you share this mindset, you'll understand the significance of the Bulgarian Bag, a unique tool that amplifies your capabilities beyond typical gym routines.

 

However, owning a Bulgarian Bag isn't enough; like with anything skill based, solid guidance is key.

 

This is a big reason I've invested so much time learning directly from Ivan Ivanov, former Olympian and Olympic coach, to bring you authentic expertise.

 

Witnessing and being a part of Ivan's coaching first hand is inspiring, to say the least, and I'm committed to sharing both the tool and the essential education behind it.

 

My journey with the Bag combines Ivan's teachings with my passion for longevity, and holistic high performance.
