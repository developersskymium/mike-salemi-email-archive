# Finding the Levity

Hi {first_name},

 

When it comes to exploring and working with things like our inner critic, limiting beliefs, and all the other aspects of our inner world, many times it can feel heavy, dense, and uncomfortable. 

For a LONG time I kept pushing to grow in a way that for the most part primarily involved an energy of seriousness and intensity - something that I still need to be mindful of at times. 

As I started finding levity in this work (side note - finding a partner who loves to play like Lauren is teaching me a lot in this regard 🙏), I realized that it is a vital medicine for life. 

As we embrace levity and play in/around deeper inner work, a few things come up with how it can be an ally:





Safety: Play can create a safe space for exploring deeper aspects of oneself, reducing fear and allowing us greater ease when stepping into something uncomfortable.
Creativity: Playfulness sparks imagination and fresh insights, unveiling hidden patterns and alternative solutions.
Emotional release: Playful activities can provide a healthy outlet for emotions, freeing us from tension and fostering processing.
Self-reflection: A lighthearted approach can encourages curiosity and openness, leading to greater self-awareness and insight.
Resilience: Playfulness can cultivate adaptability and resilience, helping us navigate challenges and embrace personal growth.
Joy and well-being: Levity injects joy, pleasure, and fun into inner work, which can make it more sustainable.
Integration: Play aids in integrating and embodying insights, bridging the gap between understanding and lasting change.

As a general theme, balancing playfulness with respect for the depth of inner work can be incredibly powerful and freeing.

As we head into the weekend I hope you are able to enjoy some light, fun activities filled with laughter alongside those people you care about most.
