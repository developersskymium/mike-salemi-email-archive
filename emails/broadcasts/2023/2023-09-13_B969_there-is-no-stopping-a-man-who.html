Hi {first_name},

 

While journaling yesterday, exploring what comes to mind when I think of an unstoppable man was the theme. And wow, I was surprised how much poured out!

 

Here is that entry...

 

"There is NO stopping a man who…

 

Is fiercely driven by his purpose.

 

Engages in regular honest self-evaluation.

 

Acts in service of his family.

 

Trusts his gut and intuition.

 

Lives from his heart.

 

Rain or shine shows up.

 

Maintains a high standard of integrity.

 

Listens intently.

 

Receives council from other wise men.

 

Is meticulous with how he speaks to himself.

 

Is physically strong and fit.

 

Knows how to regulate his own nervous system.

 

Breathes into his balls.

 

Communicates clearly

 

Recognizing when he may collapse, he still makes a heart-felt effort to remain open.

 

Takes ownership and responsibility for the life he is living and creating moment to moment.

 

Recognizes vulnerability and emotional capacity is a superpower for growth and connection.

 

Admits when he’s made a mistake, and is not too proud to apologize.

 

Hold his partner close, through the depths of her emotions.

 

Is committed.

 

Loves fully, and unapologetically.

 

Has learned that he doesn’t have to do it all himself.

 

Plays all out."

 

Sending love and respect for a great rest of your week,

Mike
