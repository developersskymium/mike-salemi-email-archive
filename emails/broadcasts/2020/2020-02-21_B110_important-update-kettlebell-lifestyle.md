WEEK 1: On Ramp 

 

Philosophy, Expectations & Results
Finding your Baseline (Intake Questionnaire)
Morning Routine
Warming Up
Working In Exercise
Stretching Review & Testing

These three elements, in addition to preparing mentally for next ~8 weeks, are truly the keys to long-term high performance.
