# The number one movement to develop dynamic rotational ability

Hi {first_name},

 

When it comes to both injury prevention as well as sports performance, there's certainly a number of different training tools and approaches one can utilize. 

 

Of all of them, however, I'd have to say the kettlebell and Suples Bulgarian Bag are definitely among my favorite top two. 

 

Compared to the Kettlebell, the Bulgarian Bag isn't as well known worldwide yet, however it is a truly a functional, high performance training tool. 

 

Designed and developed by former US Olympic Greco Roman Wrestling Coach and Bulgarian Olympian, Ivan Ivanov, the Bulgarian Bag was developed over 12 years ago and among many things, it is perfect for dynamic rotation training. The rotational (transverse) plane is arguably the most important for success in many sports, yet it is often the one least trained. Remember this - you are most susceptible to experience injury in the plane or pattern of movement that you train the least and/or are weakest in. 

 

In terms of rotational movements used with the Bulgarian Bag, the most quintessential of all is the Suples Spin. Now, after the past few years teaching Bulgarian Bag workshops and certifications, I'd have stay this is also the movement that gives people the most trouble when learning. 

 

That's why I paid a visit some months back to a friend of mine and MMA conditioning expert, Corey Beasley in Southern California to break down a few of the key progressions of this movement.

 

Click here to watch the technique breakdown tutorial.

1. Open Arm Side to Side Swing with Head and Torso Rotation

2. Half Spin

3. Full Spin

 

I hope you take a look at adding more rotational work into your training and have a blast doing so!
