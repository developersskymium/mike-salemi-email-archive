Is stretching worth it?  Or Does stretching really help? 

 

The truth of the matter is that you first have to ask yourself is, help with what? Stretching, just like any other form of exercise, is a tool to achieve a specific goal. 

 

If our goal is to deadlift 500lbs, then stretching as the ONLY means to get there will not suffice. However, it WILL HELP as an additional tool by loosening up soft tissue that can be restricting your movement thus making the lift more difficult.

 

Not everyone wants to deadlift 500lbs. But we can certainly benefit from the stretching for the same reasons of decreasing muscle tension and restricted movement.

 

This is where the longevity and performance pieces blend together.
