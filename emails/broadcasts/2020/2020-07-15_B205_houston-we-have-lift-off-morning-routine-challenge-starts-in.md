Why?

What I’ll be teaching you today is a super valuable series of techniques you can incorporate not just in the morning, but also throughout the day to help you navigate even the most challenging of situations.

 

Before we start however at 9am, I'd invite you to reflect on the following question:

 

Have you ever noticed yourself holding your breath, breathing shallowly,

or experiencing difficulty in taking a complete breath during the day?
