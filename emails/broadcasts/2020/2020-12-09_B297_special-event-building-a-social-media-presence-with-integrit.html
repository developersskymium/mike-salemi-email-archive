The problem is, many of their potential clients really don't know who they are, how special they are, and most importantly, how they have the skills and abilities that can help. 

 

Why is that?

 

Well, in my experience, a few of the reasons are:

 

  • They are busy helping people. 
  • Promoting themselves/their businesses on social media feels awkward/disingenuous to them.
  • They dislike the fakeness and flashiness that tend to be prevalent with social media.
  • They think they have to be on it all the time to be successful with it.
  • They don't understand what content to post or if how what they post could actually turn into getting them more of their ideal clients.
