Hi {first_name},

 

I don't know about you, but I used to have trouble starting my day off right.

Some days it was a struggle to "get into the groove..."

While other days, I would bounce out of bed like a lion pouncing on its prey.

Eventually, I realized that how I started my morning had a direct effect on how I felt the rest of the day.

So...over the years I've experimented with many routines to "win the day."

Everything from: meditation, working out, and visualization to affirmations, journaling, listening to music, and Qigong.

Out of everything I've tried, there was one overriding principle that led to the most success.

Whatever the routine was, it had to...

Align body, mind, and breath.

If one of these was missing, I noticed a huge decrease in the effectiveness.

If I aligned the body and the mind without the breath...

I wasn't as focused and still found myself a little flustered, especially if I was going through something stressful.

If I aligned the body and the breath without the mind...

My presence and intentions for the day were scattered.

If I aligned the breath and the mind without the body…

I would sometimes feel like a sloth that needed lubricant in his joints (although it's kind of crazy that this hasn't been the case since I've been on the carnivore diet...)

So how long does something like this take to do?

Anywhere from 5-10 minutes.

Of course, when I can, I try to do other things like journal or meditate for a longer period of time.

But, consistently doing just my foundational morning routine has been such a gamechanger for me because it is short (which makes it less intimidating to do) and primes me for the day ahead.

----------------------------------------

What does a simple example look like?

1) A glass of filtered water (I use a system from Nikken that has remineralizing stones because I'm a nerd like that :D).  Make sure to drink a bit at a time 

2) Some light movements (such as marching in place and pelvic rocks), paying special attention to expand the belly on the inhale and drawing the belly towards the spine on the exhale.

3) Box breathing - breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, and exhale for 4 seconds.  Again, paying special attention to expand the belly on the inhale and drawing the belly towards the spine on the exhale. Do this for about 2 minutes.

At each step, be mindful of how your body feels.  

Also, towards the end of the box breathing, visualize how the perfect day would go and...

Set an intention on how you want to show up.
