# [MTK] Laying the necessary foundation...the deadlift!

Laying the necessary foundation for all ballistic movements







The Deadlift

The deadlift is one of the most integral components to successful kettlebell lifting. Learning to hinge while maintaining proper spinal alignment via a stable core, not only makes this movement excellent on its own, but also magnifies its importance as a prerequisite heading into dynamic ballistic lifts such as swings, cleans and snatches.




In this section you'll be taken through a guided series of pre-checks to help you identifying any movement limitations that may exist, as well as how to address them yourself. Then, enjoy progressing into the loaded two hand deadlift. Continue onward and you'll further develop your coach's eye as we look at some of the most common errors on this lift and how to solve them. Lastly, we'll explore together a variety of deadlift variations that are presented in increasing level of difficulty and complexity. Oh, and if you're curious about programming, go to the "Tips for Training" section tab where you'll find helpful information. 




As a bonus, I've also included an additional section on the Single Arm Single Leg Deadlift. Single leg balance, pelvis, and core stability through this bend pattern movement transfers well into many work and sport environments. It's in fact a movement that shows up in many of my one-on-one client programs as well.
