Where strength and power meet

​Overhead Press and Jerk

The standing overhead strict press is one of the best upper body strength builders you can do with a kettlebell, and is the path I highly recommend starting first prior to jerks. Once your overhead press is rock solid, we'll take this strength base, and ramp up the athleticism to a high degree increasing the speed of movement, as well as incorporate a quick change of direction with the jerk.


The construction of the kettlebell, with its offset center of mass, makes it a very special training tool to use when performing overhead lifts in particular. You may find the way you grip the kettlebell and position it to be more ergonomic for the wrist than for instance a barbell or dumbbell - not to mention with a good portion of the mass of the kettlebell concentrated behind the forearm, it can also help you enter a more well aligned overhead shoulder/arm position.


The overhead press and jerk are complete with their own progressions, common error sections, variations, and programming tips for training.
