Free Bonuses

As you've seen by now, the way Mastering the Kettlebell has been designed was to provide you all the tools needed to have a truly well-rounded understanding of kettlebell training. I'm confident that the knowledge and experience gained as a result of going through this program will set you apart from 99.9% of lifters and coaches in the fitness world. 


While Mastering the Kettlebell is incredibly comprehensive, I felt I just had to give you more. Paul Chek, one of my greatest mentors and coaches, was kind enough to share an 8 part series on a fascinating topic that he calls "Working In". If you are new to this topic, it has been without a doubt the biggest "secret" I've learned over the years for long-lasting athletic performance.  Additionally, in this section you'll find a talk given by respected Upper Cervical Chiropractor and kettlebell coach Dr. Drew Vercellino, AND two more multi-part lectures on program design and posture. 


Lastly, you'll find a section titled "Performance Benchmarks". In this section are lift-specific charts categorized by gender, bodyweight, and kettlebell weight with markers to gauge your objective improvements. It may take some time, however, if you are able to complete your level 3 benchmark, you will be in a great position to take your kettlebell lifting as far as you'd like - performing a myriad of advanced lifts, and really test your grit with kettlebell challenges you may find exciting to train for in the future.


If you are a trainer looking for continuing education units, I have a great surprise for you. Very soon you'll be able to qualify for CEUs with Mastering the Kettlebell in many of the most respected Personal Training Organizations around the world. Stay tuned for an online exam coming soon you may take upon completion of the program to earn your credits.
