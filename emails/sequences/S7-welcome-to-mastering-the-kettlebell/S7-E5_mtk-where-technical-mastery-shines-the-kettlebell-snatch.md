# [MTK] Where technical mastery shines...the kettlebell snatch

Where technical mastery shines




The Snatch




The kettlebell snatch is often viewed as the most technical of all classical kettlebell lifts, and you're about to about to learn why in this section. That's not said to discourage you by any means, but rather to tell you honestly that even in my own training and with my most dedicated students, this is one particular lift that I feel never ceases to leave room for improvement. It is for this reason, the process of continually becoming a more technical, powerful, and well rounded lifter, that I LOVE the snatch and I hope you will too! 




Grip, cardio, leg development, back development, and more are all tested and conditioned during this fantastic movement. You can think of the snatch as a clean, only to a higher destination point. Thus, if you really practice your cleans in the prior section (especially with regards to "hand insertion") it will pay off here. 




This section begins with a Choosing your Snatch Technique video. Just like the clean, and in general, I suggest continuing on with the same technique (Russian or Pendulum) as utilized from the clean and swing sections for consistency purposes when learning. Following this section are snatch pre-checks, progressing into the loaded snatch, common errors, variations, and programming tips for training unique to each snatch technique.
