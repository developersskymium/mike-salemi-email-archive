The most iconic of all kettlebell movements 

The Swing

The kettlebell swing has to be the single most common movement that people think of when it comes to kettlebells, and for good reason. Due to the unique construction of the kettlebell, this tool is perfect for maximizing repetitive ballistic lifts and powerfully conditioning the entire body!


What few people know, however is that there are in fact two broad classifications of swing techniques - one you can think of as what would be needed to develop a fast sprinter, and the other related to what would be needed to train a strong 800 meter, or longer distance runner.


The first video, Choosing Your Swing Technique, aims to explain the main differences between these two, and where I suggest starting your journey. Don't over think it though, the most important thing is to start laying down reps and get started right away!


Depending on which technique you chose, you'll first head into the Pre-Checks section, similar to what you experienced with the deadlift section, except now with application and additional information geared towards the kettlebell swing. Following the pre-check section, you'll be taken into progressions for the loaded kettlebell swing (again separated by specific technique - either Russian or Pendulum).


Within the common errors section, I've explained in detail the most frequent mistakes that relate to both swing types. You will see the main tutorial related to the more popular Russian Swing, and then I provide an additionally videos demonstrating the what the Pendulum version would look like as well. 


Lastly, we go into kettlebell swing variations related to each technique. As you develop your coach's eye and experience under the bells, the difference between these two should look, and more importantly feel very different. I hope you'll enjoy these sections.


When it comes to programming, don't forget to check out the "Tips for Training" section. Here you'll find tons of helpful tips, as well as a done-for-you program unique to each swing technique.
