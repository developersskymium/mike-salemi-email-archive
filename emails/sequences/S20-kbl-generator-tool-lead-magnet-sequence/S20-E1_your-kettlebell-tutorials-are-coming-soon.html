Hi,

 

Congratulations on completing your kettlebell generator and uncovering which three kettlebells to start with.

 

Weight number one is a great choice when first getting familiar with classic kettlebell movements, as well as developing a good base level of skill.

 

Weight number two is great for most upper body lifts, as well as progressing beyond your suggested weight (number one), more focused on technique development.

 

Weight number three is excellent for lower body lifts, as well as more strength development once you've established a foundational level of proficiency.

 

If you’re only going to buy one weight, I recommend getting #2. 

 

If you get 2 of the 3 weights, choose based on your training history. 

 

New to training with weights, go for #s 1 and 2. These two weights will allow you to develop great technique safely and you’ll get plenty of muscular challenge. 

 

Experienced with weights but new to kettlebells, I recommend going with weights 2 and 3.
