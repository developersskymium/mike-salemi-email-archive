S21-E5 · The most effective fitness “secrets” are surprisingly obvious

Hello ____,

 

Watching gymnasts is amazing. They make everything look easy which is funny because what they are doing is incredibly difficult. 

 

Watching the men on the rings hold an Iron Cross - common in the gymnastics world - has made many of us men feel like we can pull that move off no problem…only to find how difficult it is. 

 

No gymnastics coach ever got a beginner gymnast and told them to go do an Iron Cross. Instead, they worked on progressions to the point where holding an Iron Cross was possible. 

 

Kettlebell Lifestyle follows the same principle. 

 

Lay the foundation > build > perform

Explanation:

The glute medius is targeted in the Side of Hip Raises which is important to stabilize the hip in single leg and lateral movement. Essential for ANYTHING athletic. The Static Alternating Side Lunge moves from an isolated movement to an integrated movement.  The Alternating Halo Side Lunge Combo adds weight, integrates the upper and lower body, becomes more dynamic, trains balance, coordination, timing etc.
