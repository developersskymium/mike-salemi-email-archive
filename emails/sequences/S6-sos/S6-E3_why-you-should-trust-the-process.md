# Why you should trust the process

Hi {first_name},




I can’t believe I’m going to share this with you…

So, essentially I’ve been through just about every kettlebell system out there, and early on I allowed myself to fall into the belief that each one was the only way to achieve the highest performance possible. All too often I would follow blindly what other so-called leaders would say and completely disregard my own inner voice and felt experience. 

I won’t say they did not provide good value, it’s just that in my experience they were very geared towards one main objective, and not utilizing the kettlebell to its fullest potential.

What I found after more than a handful of kettlebell certifications, training a ton myself, and working with respected coaches during this time, was that when seeking to develop high levels of power and strength with a kettlebell, there is a specific technique that is optimal.

On the other hand, when seeking to develop respectable levels of strength-endurance it's all about energetic efficiency, and there's in fact another, contrasting technique style that is more favorable. 

Without getting too technical, what I discovered when diving into these new ways to lift a kettlebell, my mind opened - helping me tremendously during my early development.

BUT I was still craving more, and knew I needed to level-up in order to distinguish myself as a real leader in the kettlebell industry beyond any one system or school of thought. 




Years went by, I kept on learning and refining my training techniques and application.

And then around this time I discovered Kettlebell Sport and started competing.

But everything was taken to an entirely new level when I went to the birthplace of Kettlebell training, Russia, and trained with members of the Russian National Team.

That’s when it hit me, there’s a lot more to kettlebell training, than I ever expected and had studied to that point.

I started discovering a level of depth to kettlebell training that was barely available in United States at the time.

I spent countless hours training and trying to reach the elite ranking in kettlebell sport, a ranking called “Master of Sport” but fell short competition after competition. 

The reason for my shortcoming as an athlete was not due to lack of effort but rather an injury to my left forearm that no practitioner/therapist could figure out.

After over three years of exhausting everything possible to remedy the loss of feeling and swelling that would occur in my left forearm, forcing me to set the kettlebells down during any hard effort in competition or training - I started working with Holistic Health Practitioner and rehab specialist Paul Chek.

During 2.5 years of dedicated work with Paul, I truly came to understand how complex and integrated the human body is. The rehab process involved everything from balancing my physical body to my mental-emotional self-management.




The ultimate goal was to achieve lifelong high performance, injury-free and all while loving the process.

And then finally…

I achieved the ranking “Master of Sport” in the Long Cycle event (Double 32kg Clean and Jerks in 10 minutes, performed continuously). More recently, also "Master of Sport" in Biathlon (Snatching 10 minutes and Jerking 10 minutes continuously) becoming one of only approximately six Americans to do so in both categories in the professional 32 kg kettlebell division.


After the healing process and competition success, I really wanted every kettlebell trainer in the US to know and appreciate the depth of the kettlebell holistically that I learned along my journey.

I was fortunate enough to teach many workshops and people with this new approach.

But due to physical limitations, I couldn’t achieve my goal of reaching every trainer, teaching this integrated method and preventing many of the mistakes I had made in nearly 15 years of training with kettlebells.

So I came up with another plan.

I started working on an online program, which was not easy to set-up, it took over 12 months of hard work to produce professional training videos and make something that’s not only detailed, but also helpful in many other ways related to the learning process. 

This program represents my whole life’s experience, so any trainer anytime anywhere in the world could take advantage of it and can scale their skills and business at the same time.
