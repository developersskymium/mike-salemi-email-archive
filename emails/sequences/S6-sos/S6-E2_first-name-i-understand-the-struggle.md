# {first_name}, I understand the struggle...

Hi {first_name},


As a trainer, I recall experiencing many challenges early on, and sometimes I can't believe the road it's taken to finally get here.




It's humbling looking back, and today when I see another trainers and coaches struggling with similar challenges, I can relate.

And when I say “struggling”, some of the problems I faced involved:




Attracting the right clients
How to consistently deliver more value to existing clients 
Solving complex cases
Being better with time management skills 
Learning to take care of my own health first, especially when having to work long hours




In fact, in more times than I'd like to admit, my efforts to grow my business in those days felt like I was running into a wall at times. 


Looking back I realize I was lacking clarity around my overarching dream, who I was serving, and how I could serve them best. Progress therefore felt as if  it was happening at a snail's pace, and each day felt like I was stuck living out the same routine. 


I remember putting tons of energy into the business. But still, it wasn’t giving me the outcome I desired.


After years of hard work, I understood these key components to success - aligning every part of your being with purpose, and taking care of your body so you can foster a healthier mindset is critical. With these things, I believe it is possible to achieve nearly anything. 


In my experience, these elements are also intimately connected to self-confidence. In order to grow this confidence as a trainer (once clear on why, who you're serving, how you'll serve them, and with the vitality to do all this well), in my opinion, the priority should be to master a skill set that delivers unprecedented results to these clients you seek to help.

 
If you're reading this email, more than likely you are interested in kettlebell training for health and performance - whether you are a trainer or simply an avid fitness enthusiast. With this in mind, I've created a special program for you to learn at a high-level just about everything there is to know about kettlebell training.


In my Mastering the Kettlebell Program I give you a road map on how to move step-by-step towards mastering this incredible tool (for developing strength, power, endurance, flexibility, and coordination) to become a true leader in fitness and performance training.


Developing the authentic knowledge and experience that went into it wasn't easy. In fact, for a number of reasons I almost quit, many times...but I'm forever grateful that I didn't because after so many trials and errors I finally created something that changed the game for me as a teacher and the results of my clients. 


Once I got to a place where I felt I had enough real world knowledge and application that I could deliver everyday at the highest level, more devoted clients started seeking me out for training. Life started becoming more and more interesting and enjoyable.  


Are you interested in achieving similar, or even better results?


If so, then wait for my email tomorrow. In it, I’m going to show you exactly how you can do it!


Regards,
Mike Salemi
