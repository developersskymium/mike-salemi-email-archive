# Complexity with strength and grace...the Turkish Get Up

Complexity with strength and grace

Turkish Get Up




Next to the kettlebell swing, the Turkish Get Up is arguably the lift that people love most in kettlebell training. It is a full body movement that trains pressing, rotation, bending, and lunging, AND involves all three planes of movement - sagittal (front-to-back), frontal (side-to-side), and transverse (rotation). If there was ever a total body big-bang kettlebell exercise it is this. 




Due to its complexity, there is a tremendous amount of information you are going to learn about yourself and gain from practicing this movement. To start, you'll be taken through the pre-check section, progressions into the bodyweight and loaded movement (along with bonus videos like a neat challenge!), common errors, variations and programming tips for training.




As an added bonus, included is another movement (the windmill), with full video and text breakdowns. The kettlebell windmill is not only an excellent movement for functional core stability, but also it trains the shoulder and hip complex like no other!




Have fun enjoying this movement and the associated sections in the program.
