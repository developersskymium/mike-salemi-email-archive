Let's get our lower body and pulling training on!

Squatting, Lunging and Rowing

In my opinion, the squat and lunge may be two of the most underrated movements when it comes to kettlebell training. The movements patterns associated with them are absolutely critical in daily life, and in many work and sport environments - not to mention the nature of the kettlebell makes it incredibly easy to scale progressions/regressions. Whether it's by utilizing a different grip or orientation of the kettlebell, there is a ton of variety and benefits you can experience by implementing kettlebells into your lower body training. 


In the program there are complete sections on squatting, lunging, and rowing. You'll find tabs for each movement with pre-checks, progressing into loaded movements, common errors, and variations. While each of these three movements contain programming tips for training, I have written a full done-for-you program focused on the squat. This will set you up for successful squatting well into the future with many lower body movements; not to mention help you reach your performance benchmarks...stay tuned for the next email to learn more about this! ; )
