A fantastic lower and upper body integrated movement

The Clean

The kettlebell clean is the movement where we truly start integrating the lower and upper body. This lift tends to give beginners the most trouble due to a new technical element being introduced called "hand insertion" - where the hand fully inserts through the kettlebell window as we enter the rack position. Often times when done incorrectly, the kettlebell will painfully bang on-top of the forearms. Step-by-step you'll learn helpful progressions to expedite the learning curve; just remember that learning a new skill like this takes time. Trust the process and it will get smoother and smoother, I promise!


This section is laid out similarly to our prior swing section and begins with a Choosing your Clean Technique video. In general, I suggest continuing on with the same technique (Russian or Pendulum) as you utilize during the swing section to keep things simple and consistent for the time being. Following this section are clean pre-checks, progressing into the loaded clean, common errors, variations, and programming tips for training unique to each technique. 


If you happened to skip straight to this section when opening the program, I highly suggest watching the Choosing your Clean video first. It will put into context and guide you best especially as you move through all the ballistic movements - swings, cleans and, snatches.
